# Launch Pad Year 3 (The Helm) - Session 7 "The Council" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft | **When:** weeks 25-30 (long session, 6 weeks; the year's - and the arc's - harvest)
**Companions:** Year 3 studio-year plan (Helm); Helm Sessions 1-6; three-year spec (Season Three); coach-not-therapist referral pathway

## The session at a glance
- **Arc:** OPEN (a council is not a debate) -> DEEPEN (reason from principle; name the cost; honor dissent; the Kobayashi Maru move) -> APPLY (build and rehearse the deliberation) -> HARVEST (a public Council on a real dilemma, before a warm audience).
- **The thread:** *a council is not a debate to win. It is people of good faith reasoning together toward the best hard call - naming the cost, honoring the dissent, and, when the choice is a false binary, refusing it to find the third option (the Kobayashi Maru move: change the terms of the test).*
- **The structure to teach (simply):** the dilemma -> reason from principle -> name what it costs -> honor the dissent -> the third option, if there is one. **The exhibition IS the reasoning, not a verdict to be judged.**
- **Helm rhythm:** Tue guided-lighter (council craft, deliberation games, rehearsal in the tribe - toddler-flexible), Thu guided-deep (the Socratic shaping of the real deliberation). Mon/Wed self-guided; Fri fallow.

## Care points (this is one of the three heaviest sessions)
- **SECOND ADULT REQUIRED on the deep days of the real deliberation and the public Council** - public moral deliberation is exposure on exposure; the second adult holds any learner who needs to step out.
- **The dilemma is allegorical/external, never personal** - the Council deliberates a real moral question (a case, a policy, a historical hard call), never a learner's own life. No forced self-disclosure.
- **The Council that cannot be failed, somatically (K'Ehleyr):**
  - a **pass signal** (a word or gesture that returns the floor with zero cost) - freeze is an autonomic state, not a choice; make it safe;
  - **ground-before-you-speak** taught as the captain's own craft (feet on the floor, one breath) - regulation as competence, not remediation;
  - the audience is **witness, not judge** - brief the witnesses; a room of warm faces is co-regulation at scale;
  - the **feast is the come-down** - protect it as the discharge, not one more thing to get right (eating together, in company, is the body's signal that the danger is over).
- **Somatic floor:** open settle -> mid-work discharge -> close return; pre-brief before the Council; battle-buddy pairing and the physical exit ramp in place.
- Coach-not-therapist carries; referral pathway live.

## Week 25 - OPEN (a council is not a debate)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core / HS credits; a hard question your community faces with no clean answer; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - what a council is (vs a debate); reasoning together, not winning; the cohort chooses the real dilemma it will deliberate. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; research the chosen dilemma; where do you land, and why? Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - reason from principle; name the cost of every path; the discipline of honoring dissent. Bridge: find the strongest version of the view you disagree with.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 26 - DEEPEN (the Kobayashi Maru move)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; is the dilemma a true binary, or a false one? Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - deliberation games; steelmanning; the Kobayashi Maru move - refusing a false binary to change the terms of the test. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; draft your principled position (principle, cost, dissent honored, third option if any); Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - shape the deliberation together; where it reasons well, where it collapses into debate. Bridge: toward a full run.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Weeks 27-28 - APPLY (build and rehearse the deliberation)
Each week, same shape:
- **Mon / Wed - Self-guided** - core; refine your reasoning and role in the Council; Log; check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - council-craft; ground-before-you-speak practice; the pass signal rehearsed; low-stakes run-throughs. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - full rehearsal in the tribe's safety; the guide holds reasoning over performance; hold nerves warmly. Bridge: toward the public Council.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 29 - APPLY (final rehearsal; set the room)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; polish your part / chosen role (full Council / smaller role / witness - each honored); Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - dry-run; confirm each learner's chosen level; brief the witness-audience (witness, not judge). *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; a Log entry on the year and the arc; rest. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep), SECOND ADULT** - final dry-run; ground the room; the pass signal and exit ramp confirmed; the feast planned as the come-down. Bridge: rest before the Council.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 30 - HARVEST (the public Council + feast)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; final touches; rest. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - set the room for the Council and feast; last nerves held. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - final prep; rest. Check-in.
- **Thu - THE COUNCIL + FEAST, SECOND ADULT** - a public deliberation of a real dilemma before a warm witness-audience: reason from principle, name the cost, honor dissent, and (if the binary is false) find the third option. *The exhibition is the reasoning, never a verdict; a learner may pass, pause, or come back with zero cost.* The feast is the come-down and the celebration. This is The Helm's harvest.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 7 and the Helm year.**

## The year-close and the Launch (carry the operating plan)
Before the next season - or, for a graduating captain, before the Launch: a **co-authoring close** (the learner reviews the year's design *with* the guide - "here is how The Helm was built, and why; tell me what to change") and a **Captain's Log ownership handoff** ("it is yours; take it; no copy kept").

**For the founding cohort completing the cycle - the Next Great Adventure (the Launch), sequenced:** guide opens the rite (never the system) -> look back and read the Growth Record together (arrive at where you have been) -> **the grief-and-gratitude beat** (the ending is allowed to be sad, not only exciting - invited, never assigned) -> the Dome opens, the Star Chart revealed once -> craft the next-step document -> **the handoff** (generate the Growth Record Portrait export, choose its visibility, take it - this beat cannot be ceremony over a placeholder; the v1 export must be real) -> the Launch (she leaves; the door closes and stays unlocked from her side; no come-back nudge). The learner walks out holding three things: the Growth Record (backward face), the next-step document (forward face), and the Captain's Log (their private voice). *The arc completes.*

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
