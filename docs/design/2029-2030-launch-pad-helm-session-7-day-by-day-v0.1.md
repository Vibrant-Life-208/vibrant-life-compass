## The Council as a real deliberative form (Curzon) - build the form, or it becomes debate
"They deliberate publicly" is a hope, not a container. Without a form, teenagers in front of an audience default to what school rehearsed: debate to win. The form:
- **Named, rotating roles:** *convener* (holds the question, not a position), *voices* (argue the live positions in good faith), *scribe* (writes the reasoning where the room can see it), and the **steward of the absent** ("who is affected by this who is not in the room?" - the decision measured at its margins).
- **A six-phase sequence:** Frame -> Divergence (surface every position and the fear under it, *before* arguing) -> **Steelman** -> the Cost Ledger (every path's cost and who pays, on the board) -> the Turn (real binary or false? - the Third Chair) -> the Sense of the Room (where the reasoning arrived, and where it honestly did not).
- **The Steelman, mandatory and physical:** you may not argue your own position until you have voiced the best version of the one you reject, well enough that its holder says "yes, that's it." This is how honoring dissent is built into the bones (Forum Theatre from S4 pays off here).
- **The Third Chair (the false-binary move, made teachable):** an empty chair between hardened sides; four questions - what is each side protecting? is the "or" real or inherited? what would honor *both* values at once? and, when there is no third option, choose from principle and carry the grief honestly.
- **A scribe and a board (the load-bearing hook):** the reasoning written where all can see becomes the object in the room, not the performers. **The strongest unresolved dissent is read into the record in the dissenter's own words and left standing, unrefuted, as part of the harvest.**
- **Consecrate and release (Bareil):** a marked opening ("this is not ordinary talk"), and a **closing that completes the council without resolving the dilemma** ("we did not solve it; we reasoned honestly, together, in front of our people; the council is closed") - so a young person can carry an unresolved hard thing without it becoming an open wound.
- **Real stakes without a verdict (Kirk):** the Council deliberates something with actual consequence to Vibrant Life or Boise - a real policy or trade-off the community faces - so the reasoning matters because it is *used*, not *graded*.

## Care points (this is one of the three heaviest sessions)
- **SECOND ADULT REQUIRED on the deep days of the real deliberation and the public Council** - public moral deliberation is exposure on exposure; the second adult holds any learner who needs to step out.
- **The dilemma is allegorical/external, never personal** - the Council deliberates a real moral question (a case, a policy, a historical hard call), never a learner's own life. No forced self-disclosure.
- **The Council that cannot be failed, somatically (K'Ehleyr):**
  - a **pass signal** (a word or gesture that returns the floor with zero cost) - freeze is an autonomic state, not a choice; make it safe;
  - **ground-before-you-speak** taught as the captain's own craft (feet on the floor, one breath) - regulation as competence, not remediation;
  - the audience is **witness, not judge** - brief the witnesses; a room of warm faces is co-regulation at scale;
  - the **feast is the come-down** - protect it as the discharge, not one more thing to get right (eating together, in company, is the body's signal that the danger is over).
- **Somatic floor:** open settle -> mid-work discharge -> close return; pre-brief before the Council; battle-buddy pairing and the physical exit ramp in place.
- Coach-not-therapist carries; referral pathway live.

This is The Helm's harvest.

## The year-close and the Launch (carry the operating plan)
Before the next season - or, for a graduating captain, before the Launch: a **co-authoring close** (the learner reviews the year's design *with* the guide - "here is how The Helm was built, and why; tell me what to change") and a **Captain's Log ownership handoff** ("it is yours; take it; no copy kept").

**For the founding cohort completing the cycle - the Next Great Adventure (the Launch), built as a true rite of passage (Bareil + Guinan + Ezri):**

- **SEPARATION (the un-belonging):** guide opens the rite (never the system) -> look back and read the Growth Record together (arrive at where you have been) -> **the grief-and-gratitude beat** as the *work* of this phase (the ending named directly - "this was your home; you are being released from it" - allowed to be sad, held, unrushed, with a real exit). *Do not de-role this grief* - it is stepping out of a self that truly was you, not a role that never was.
- **LIMINALITY (the threshold, held):** the Dome opens; the **Star Chart given as a threshold-object in a silence no one fills** - the map of where they have been, handed at the moment they must navigate by it alone -> craft the next-step document.
- **REINCORPORATION (the crossing, witnessed, with a change of standing):** **the handoff** (generate the Growth Record Portrait export, choose its visibility, take it - this beat cannot be ceremony over a placeholder; the v1 export must be real) + the **Captain's Log** handed over ("it is yours; no copy kept") + the **Mantle keep-or-leave** choice (either true; the choosing is the rite) + **leaving a gift for the room** (a word to the next captain, a mark, their Vineyard-thing - for the founding cohort, the room's first inheritance) + a **witnessed crossing** over a literal threshold into a circle of adults who receive them *as an adult and address them differently on the far side* -> the Launch (the door closes and stays unlocked from her side; no come-back nudge).

The cohort marks the departure *as a cohort* (not the guide performing it at them). **The family-less learner is named and held** - the tribe is *your people*, said in advance, the inclusion floor applied to the most tender day of nine years. The learner walks out holding three things: the Growth Record (backward face), the next-step document (forward face), and the Captain's Log (their private voice). *The arc completes.*

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
