# Launch Pad Year 2 (The Forge) - Session 6 "Hands to Work" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft | **When:** weeks 19-24 (long session, 6 weeks; the finish line)
**Companions:** Year 2 studio-year plan (Forge); Forge Sessions 1-5; three-year spec (Season Two)

## The session at a glance
- **Arc:** OPEN (the service-choice menu) -> DEEPEN (empathy; design *with*) -> APPLY (carry out the project, with real people) -> HARVEST (a real action taken).
- **The thread:** *assigned service teaches compliance; chosen service teaches contribution. Design *with* the people who have the problem, never *for* them.*
- **The menu (chosen, never assigned):** direct service / advocacy / philanthropy / building / teaching.
- **Lighter-Forge week:** Tue guided-lighter (the doing/building/service prep + service expeditions - toddler-flexible), Thu guided-deep (empathy, design-with, reflection). Mon/Wed self-guided; Fri fallow.

## Care points
- **Safety:** do not place teenagers in emotionally over-their-head situations with vulnerable populations without support. Service should **stretch, not wound.** Match the mode to the learner's readiness; the guide (and any partner adult) stays close.
- **Service expeditions are off-site** - toddler coverage travels/planned per outing (as in S4).
- **Reciprocity (from the Closing Feast lens):** if service touches a community, it honors and includes, never uses; design *with*, and let the benefit flow to the people served.
- Coach-not-therapist and deficit-guard carry.

## Week 19 - OPEN (choose your mode)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; which mode of serving fits you and your problem? Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; **the service-choice menu** presented - direct service / advocacy / philanthropy / building / teaching; each learner chooses. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; plan your action; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - empathy as the core skill; design *with*, never *for*; who are the real people with this problem? Bridge: talk to one of them.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 20 - DEEPEN (design with)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; talk to a real, affected person; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; service prep / building in company. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; shape your action from what real people told you; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - design-with in practice; adjust your plan to the people, not your assumptions. Bridge: begin the real action.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Weeks 21-23 - APPLY (carry out the project, with real people)
Each week, same shape:
- **Mon / Wed - Self-guided** - carry out the service / philanthropy / building / teaching project, with real people; core; Log; check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; hands-on project work / service expeditions in company. *Toddler-flexible; off-site outings planned.*
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - reflect on what's happening in the real action; iterate; hold anything heavy (coach-not-therapist). Bridge: the next real step.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 24 - HARVEST (a real action taken)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; finish and consolidate your action; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; share what you did and what happened. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; reflect: did it leave people better off? Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): HARVEST** - a **real action taken, with real people** - and the honest reflection ("did this serve, or did I just feel good?"). Voluntary share. *Empty-basket honored: an action that didn't land as hoped is data about the thing, never a verdict on you.* Bridge into Session 7 (The Call to Action): *"You did the work. Now: make a room care about it."*
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 6.**

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
