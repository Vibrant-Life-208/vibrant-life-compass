# Launch Pad Year 3 (The Helm) - Session 6 "The Bridge" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft | **When:** weeks 22-24 (short session)
**Companions:** Year 3 studio-year plan (Helm); Helm Sessions 1-5; three-year spec (Season Three)

## The session at a glance
- **Arc:** OPEN (what leadership actually is) -> DEEPEN (rotating command; find-your-style; the hard parts) -> HARVEST (a real act of leading peers - and of being led).
- **The thread:** *leadership is stewardship, not command. The best captains lead toward their own unnecessity - they build a crew that no longer needs them. You practice both seats: leading, and being led well.*
- **The work:** rotating command (everyone takes the chair); find-your-captain-style (which captain do you lead like - and which do you want to grow toward?); leadership as service; the hard parts (holding a decision, disappointing someone, being disagreed with); honoring the quiet leaders (leadership is not volume).
- **Helm rhythm:** Tue guided-lighter (leadership games, rotating command, expedition leadership - toddler-flexible), Thu guided-deep (the hard parts of leading; stewardship). Mon/Wed self-guided; Fri fallow.
- **Collaborative and safe by design** - no second adult needed.

## Care points
- **Leadership is stewardship, not dominance** - guard against the room rewarding the loudest; name and honor the quiet leaders (the one who makes others better, the one who notices who is left out).
- **Being led is a skill too** - a learner who only ever leads has not finished the lesson; rotate genuinely.
- **The hard parts are held, not performed** - disappointing someone, holding an unpopular call, being disagreed with; coach the internal weight, not the optics.
- **Mixed-age care:** do not let the graduating captains' weight set a ceiling the younger learners measure against; every seat in the chair is honored equally.
- Carry the somatic settle and glad-close. Coach-not-therapist carries.

- **Mon - Self-guided** - core / HS credits; a leader who changed something for you - what did they actually do? Log. Check-in.

- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; when have you led? When have you been led well? Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - leadership as service; the crew that no longer needs you as the goal, not a loss. Bridge: take the chair this week.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 23 - DEEPEN (find your style; the hard parts)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; take a turn leading a peer task; notice your instincts; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - find-your-captain-style (which captain do you lead like?); expedition leadership - plan and lead a real outing together. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; a time leading asked something hard of you; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - the hard parts: holding a decision, disappointing someone, being disagreed with; honoring the quiet leaders. Bridge: what kind of captain are you becoming?
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 24 - HARVEST (leading, and being led)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; prepare your real act of leading (a task, a project, an outing); Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - the cohort runs a self-led challenge; every learner takes a seat (lead or be led, both honored). *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; reflect: what did leading teach that being led did not, and the reverse? Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): HARVEST** - a real act of leading peers *and* of being led well - the two seats, both honored. *Empty-basket honored: "I found I lead best quietly, from beside not in front" is a real finding, not a lesser one.* Bridge into Session 7 (The Council): *"You can reason, empathize, and lead. Now: make a hard call in the open, before your people."*
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 6.**
