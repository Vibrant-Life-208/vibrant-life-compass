*What a week actually looks like. The order below is the real one, lifted from the guide's own book rather than written for you.*

---

### The week

**Tuesday and Thursday are guide mornings.** The guide runs the studio with you, and the order of the morning is the same every single time, so nobody walks in wondering what today is.

**Monday and Wednesday are your own.** You run your own morning: your reading, your core academics at your own pace, your personal project, the session's writing. Nobody is steering but you.

The honest part of those two days: the guide is in the building, at Vibrant Life Tots with the younger children from 9:15 to 11:30, and comes to you around noon for a real check-in. Same site, different room. If you need them before noon, you go and get them. You are genuinely running your own hours, and you are never alone in the building. Both are true at once.

**Friday is fallow.** Deliberately empty. A fallow field is one left unplanted on purpose, because that is what makes it rich again. It is for rest, play, friendship, and making things for no reason, and it is never earned, never filled, and never converted back into work to cover a busy week.

Every morning runs 9:00 to 1:00.

---

### A guided morning, start to finish

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 9:00 - 9:25 | **The walk-in.** Door open, nothing required. Put your things down, make tea, talk or don't. The guide is here and will find one or two people for a quiet one-to-one. |
| 9:25 - 9:35 | **The settle, then the circle.** Three breaths, the same words every time. Then one round of "how are you, really?" You may pass. |
| 9:35 - 10:50 | **The Work of the Day.** The session's main work. This is the part that changes week to week. The rest of this table does not. |
| 10:50 - 11:00 | **The way down.** Stand, shake it out, step outside, water. |
| 11:00 - 11:40 | **The Table.** We eat together. You bring your own lunch. |
| 11:40 - 12:40 | **Hands and each other.** Making, cooking, building, fixing, an errand into the world, a project with somebody else. Different from the morning's work on purpose: the body works and the talking gets easy. |
| 12:40 - 1:00 | **The close.** One light thing, then your closing minute, then you go. |

**The times are honest rather than strict.** Most days drift ten minutes. The order never drifts.

---

### Two things inside the day that are easy to miss

**The Table is not a lunch break.** We stop, sit down together, and the guide sits and eats with you. You bring your own lunch; on ordinary days the studio does not provide food. The forty minutes is for being a group of people who eat together rather than getting through food on the way to the next thing. You sit wherever you want and no seat is assigned.

**The closing minute is yours.** It ends every guide day, and what is said in it is not collected, graded or reported.
