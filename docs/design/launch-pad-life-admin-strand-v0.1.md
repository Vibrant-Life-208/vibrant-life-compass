# The Launch Pad - Life Admin Strand (do-it-for-real, just-in-time)

**A cross-year companion strand for the 16-18 studio - the practical "adulting" layer**
**Status:** v0.1 working draft - from the 2026-08 Europa + Cura brainstorm; pressure-tested live
**Runs:** all three years (Origin / The Forge / The Helm), on the self-guided project days, front-loaded into the launch year
**Companions:** the three studio-year plans; Helm S5 "The Arena of Voices" (real adults); the Growth Record; the existing dark-shipped Compass Life Skills / Financial Literacy courses

> This is not a course. It is the practical life-admin layer the Socratic studio work otherwise never touches - taxes, insurance, a lease, the documents that are you - delivered the way Compass says learning actually sticks: *do it for real, once, with a guide beside you, at the moment it is actually due.*

## The design principle

Most life-skills curricula teach that taxes exist. The real gap is not knowledge - it is the terror of the first form, done alone, with real money on the line. That is the Compass thesis exactly: **the gap is discipline and doing, not knowledge.** Nobody stumbles because they did not know insurance existed; they stumble because the first time they had to pick a plan there was no one beside them and the stakes were real.

So the strand's whole shape is:
- **Do it for real, not a class about it.** "Today we file your taxes together" beats a Financial Literacy unit every time - a real artifact, a real task, a guide beside you.
- **Just-in-time, not just-in-case.** Each skill is tied to the moment it is actually needed. A comprehensive "here is all of adulthood" course glazes eyes; the same content at the moment of need sticks for life.
- **Learner-pulled, guide-offered.** The learner's real life surfaces the need (at the ~12:00 check-in); the guide offers the real task. Not pushed on a schedule.

## The three triggers (how "now" gets decided)

Just-in-time only works if something reliably fires the "now" signal. Three ways a life-skill becomes due:

1. **Life-event triggered** (per-learner, asynchronous). The learner hits a real threshold and the check-in catches it: *"I got a job offer" -> this week we do your real W-4 and read your first pay stub.* *"We're getting an apartment" -> we read your actual lease line by line.* Got a car, turned 18, applying to college, a health scare - each pulls its task. This is the spine.
2. **Calendar-anchored** (cohort-wide, seasonal). Some things have a real clock: tax season (Jan-Apr), FAFSA (opens October), voter registration before an election, open enrollment. These become shared project-day clusters at the real moment - everyone files a return in March, once, together.
3. **Launch-anchored** (final year). The cluster *everyone* needs before they walk out, front-loaded into The Helm: the documents audit, the health-coverage transition, the full independent-living stack, the Launch-Floor sweep (below).

## The "do it for real" ladder (because most learners are minors)

A 16-year-old cannot sign a lease. So "for real" scales three ways, and the guide picks the highest rung that is legal-and-real for that learner:
- **Real now, any age:** open a real bank account, read a real pay stub, track a real month's budget, get a state ID, register to vote at 17.5, spot a real phishing email, cancel a real subscription, read a real Terms of Service.
- **Real artifact, simulated stakes:** file a *realistic* return with the actual IRS forms and sample (or their own) numbers; walk the real ACA marketplace and price real plans; read a real lease.
- **Real at the threshold:** their *actual* first-job W-4, their *actual* FAFSA, their *actual* apartment lease - done with the guide the week it lands.

**Privacy floor:** "do it for real" defaults to the learner's *own* real life plus realistic simulations. Family data (the family's lease, the family's taxes) is touched only with the family's consent - the learner's own bank account is theirs; the family's finances are not.

## The Launch Floor - eight things, done once, before you go

The Floor is the small, non-negotiable set nobody should leave without having done *once*. Each earns its place only by clearing four bars: **high cost to stumble on, near-universal regardless of path, genuinely paralyzing the first time alone, and easy to dodge until it bites you.** Everything else stays genuinely just-in-time. The Floor is **offered as care - part of being handed the keys - never graded, never a deadline, never ranked.** "Before you go, we make sure you have touched these once, together."

1. **Your paycheck & taxes.** Read a real pay stub (gross vs net, FICA, deductions); file one real or realistic return. *(A refund means you overpaid.)* Do-it: file via Free File.
2. **Banking & credit - money you hold and owe.** Hold a real account; pull and read your own free credit report; understand how a score is built from zero and how credit-card interest compounds against you. Do-it: open/hold an account; read your real credit report.
3. **Money that grows.** Compound interest, start-early (time is the one asset an 18-year-old has), index-over-stock-picking, the employer match (free money) - and the inoculation: active trading / meme-stocks / crypto are the casino, not investing. Do-it: model your own compound-growth curve; identify a real index fund and the match on a real (or sample) offer.
4. **Health coverage.** How you are covered the day you leave, how to get covered (employer, ACA marketplace, Medicaid, a parent's plan to 26); decode premium / deductible / copay / out-of-pocket / network; urgent care vs ER. Do-it: walk the real marketplace and price a plan; map your own path.
5. **Renting.** Read a real lease line by line; tenant rights vs responsibilities; the first+last+deposit wall; renters insurance exists and is cheap. Do-it: read a real lease and flag the traps.
6. **The documents that are you.** Locate, secure, and know how to replace your birth certificate, Social Security card, ID/license (and passport). Do-it: the personal-documents audit.
7. **Spot the trap.** Recognize phishing, subscription cancel-traps, predatory lending, get-rich-quick schemes, and identity theft - and know what to do when hit. *(Evoked's own home turf.)* Do-it: dissect a real phishing email and a real cancel-flow; know the fraud-response steps.
8. **The first-job paperwork & your rights.** Fill a real W-4 (and know the I-9, direct deposit); at-will employment and your basic workplace rights; read a job offer for total comp and the match. Do-it: complete a real W-4; decode a real or sample offer.

**The connective tissue (not a ninth item, the muscle under all eight):** *make one real call to a bureaucracy* - a benefits line, the DMV, an insurer - and get an answer. That single experience dissolves the terror-of-the-form for good, and builds the meta-disposition the whole strand is really teaching: a form is just a form; you can call and ask; you can find the right person before you sign; asking for help is not shame.

## Deliberately NOT on the Floor (the judgment calls)

- **Interpersonal growth / relationships / confidence / goal-setting** - off the Floor on purpose; it is the *entire three-year spine* of the Launch Pad (the Socratic, leadership, Council, Vineyard arc). Putting it on the admin Floor would both duplicate the core work and shrink something huge into a checkbox.
- **FAFSA / college enrollment** - universal *only if college-bound*; fails the "regardless of path" bar. Stays just-in-time (calendar-anchored, October).
- **Active trading / stock-picking / crypto, car-buying, entrepreneurship/LLC, travel, home-buying** - path-dependent or self-triggering; genuinely just-in-time, never Floor.
- **Government workings (civics)** - "how government is structured" is knowledge-delivery; the *practical* half (applying, the documents, the real call) is already on the Floor. See the enrichment strand below.

## The just-in-time reference library (the existing courses, reframed)

The dark-shipped Compass Life Skills and Financial Literacy courses are **not** thrown out and **not** something a learner sits through. They become the **reference manual the learner opens the moment they need it**, standing beside the real task. The strand is the doing-with-a-guide; the courses are the how-to you reach for mid-doing. This gives the existing build its right role and wastes nothing.

## Enrichment & path-dependent strands (real, valued - just not mandatory)

These run purely just-in-time / by interest, never as a Floor:

- **Grants & the shame-free ask.** Not Floor (path-dependent), but elevated above ordinary enrichment because it teaches the exact opposite disposition to predatory lending: *legitimate free money exists and you are allowed to ask for it.* Real do: write and submit one real scholarship or small-grant application. **Curriculum-lived:** the guide is, in real life, running Evoked's own grant search - modeling a real grant hunt is the sibling of the toddler-in-orbit lesson.
- **Government / civics, the reachable kind.** Anchored on the truth that *local government touches your life most and is the most reachable.* Real do: contact a real representative about a real issue, or sit in on one real local meeting (city council, school board). Where-your-taxes-go and local-vs-state-vs-federal ride along as context, not as a lecture.
- **The wider world of work - jobs you have never heard of.** Deliberate mind-opening: you cannot choose a path you do not know exists. Surface unusual, obscure, and unexpected careers (the person whose whole job is a thing no one at the table knew was a job). Pairs directly with S5's guest speakers - the most mind-opening visitor is rarely the doctor. Real do: each learner researches one genuinely surprising career and brings it to the cohort; the guest-speaker roster deliberately includes at least one unexpected vocation. This is joy-adjacent (Vineyard-flavored) - wonder at how many ways there are to make a life.
- **The rest, on demand:** FAFSA, car-buying, investing beyond the basics, entrepreneurship/LLC, travel logistics, home-buying - opened the moment a learner's real life reaches for them.

## Real adults (plugs into Helm S5)

The strand is the natural home for the S5 real-adult work: a real accountant walks tax day; a tenant-rights person walks lease-reading; a careful (non-sales) insurance person walks health coverage; an unexpected vocation opens the wider-world-of-work strand. All under the gates already designed - Riker's safeguarding (guide present / observable channel / vetting proportional to access) and Kasidy's reciprocity (real honoraria, the story is a gift never the price, close the loop).

## The readiness map (completeness without a deadline)

The honest tension: pure just-in-time risks a learner launching having never triggered "taxes" or "health coverage." The fix is **not** a graded checklist (that violates the six refusals). Instead:

- A **learner-owned readiness map** - a menu of the real-world tasks (Floor + just-in-time + enrichment), that the learner drives as life triggers them. Lives in the Compass Life Skills tab; feeds the Growth Record; the Captain's Log holds the reflection. It is a *map they own*, not a compliance tracker the guide checks off.
- The **launch-year sweep** ensures the eight-item Floor is touched once - offered as part of being handed the keys, gently ("what is still unopened?"), never as an audit, a deadline, or a grade. That is how sovereignty and a real floor coexist.

## Six-refusals compliance

No grades, no ranking, no banking-model deposit, no external deadline, no productivity-as-worth, no surveillance. The Floor is offered as care; the readiness map is learner-owned; the sweep is an invitation, not an audit. A learner who launches having chosen not to open something they were genuinely offered has exercised sovereignty - the Floor guarantees the *offer*, not the compliance.

## Preconditions & open items

1. **Build the readiness map** in the Compass Life Skills tab (learner-owned; feeds the Growth Record; not a graded tracker).
2. **Assemble the do-it-for-real kits** - the Free File walkthrough, the ACA-marketplace walk, a sample lease, a sample job offer, a real phishing/cancel-flow teardown, the documents-audit checklist, the compound-growth model.
3. **The real-adult roster** for tax day / lease / insurance / unexpected vocations - under the S5 safeguarding + reciprocity gates (Riker, Kasidy; George for outreach and honoraria).
4. **Legal-for-minors pass** - confirm which "real" tasks a minor can do independently vs. which need the real-artifact/simulated rung or family consent.
5. **The privacy line** - own-data default; family data only with family consent.
6. **Sequencing table** - map each item to its trigger type (life-event / calendar / launch), so the guide knows what to watch for and when.

*"We evoke - we never extract." - the world is bigger than the ten jobs a teenager has heard of, and the first tax form is only terrifying once. Do it for real, together, at the moment it is due.*
