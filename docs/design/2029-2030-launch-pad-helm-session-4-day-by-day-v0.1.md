# Launch Pad Year 3 (The Helm) - Session 4 "The Stage" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.2 working draft (fresh-eyes elevation) | **When:** weeks 13-18 (long session, 6 weeks)
**Companions:** Year 3 studio-year plan (Helm); Helm Sessions 1-3; three-year spec (Season Three); coach-not-therapist referral pathway

## The session at a glance
- **Arc:** OPEN (theatre as perspective-taking) -> DEEPEN (monologue work; archetypes as doorways; *Our Town*) -> APPLY (Forum Theatre - rehearse a hard call on your feet) -> HARVEST (a performed moment of empathy - stepping fully into another life and back out whole).
- **The thread:** *to make a hard call well you have to feel from inside the lives it touches. Theatre is the safest way to practice standing in someone else's shoes - and the discipline of stepping back out is as important as stepping in.*
- **The material:** monologue work; archetypes as *doorways not identities* (you try a way of being on, you are not it); *Our Town* ("do any human beings ever realize life while they live it? - every, every minute?"); Forum Theatre (Boal - the audience can stop the scene and change the choice); and the de-roling ritual after every embodiment.
- **Helm rhythm:** Tue guided-lighter (theatre-as-play, improv, warm-ups - toddler-flexible), Thu guided-deep (monologue, *Our Town*, Forum Theatre). Mon/Wed self-guided; Fri fallow. The Vineyard runs alongside.
- **A note on joy (Guinan):** the theatre-as-play here is genuine delight, not only a safety warm-up - protect it as such; keep the Vineyard (joy for its own sake) distinct from the de-roling toolkit, which is safety craft.

## Care points (this is one of the three heaviest sessions)
- **SECOND ADULT REQUIRED on the *Our Town* death-scene deep day** - it is the one place the season's allegory shield does not apply, because death on the stage *is* the real thing, and a grieving learner takes a direct hit. The second adult's only job is to leave with the learner who needs to leave.
- **Know which learners are grieving before the death scene runs** - a recent loss makes Emily's return a direct hit, not allegory. This is a pre-loaded judgment, written before the day, never improvised. Name a previewed exit for that scene specifically.
- **De-roling after EVERY embodiment, with a real mechanic** (K'Ehleyr) - the nervous system does not know a role is pretend; it runs the state for real. After each embodiment: say your own name, name three things true about your real life right now ("I am [name], I am 17, it is Tuesday, I have soccer after this"), physically remove the role/prop, shake out the hands, feel the floor. Not once per session - every time a body wears another life.
- **Roles are chosen, opt-out always, archetypes are doorways not identities** - no learner is assigned a role that lands wrong on them; a role can be given to the tribe only, observed rather than performed, or declined.
- **Somatic floor:** open settle -> mid-work discharge -> close return, every heavy day; pre-brief before the death scene (arrival, not ambush); battle-buddy pairing and the physical exit ramp in place before the scene opens.
- Coach-not-therapist carries; referral pathway live.

## Week 13 - OPEN (theatre as perspective-taking)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core / HS credits; whose shoes have you never stood in? Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - theatre warm-ups, improv, play; the body as an instrument of empathy; low-stakes, glad. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; choose a life very different from yours to explore; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - perspective-taking through a first embodiment; archetypes as doorways (try it on, you are not it); the first de-roling. Bridge: find a monologue that is not yours.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 14 - DEEPEN (monologue work)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; sit with your chosen monologue - whose voice is it? Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - monologue play; voice, body, intention; low-stakes read-throughs. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; what does this character want that you have never wanted? Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - monologue work in the room (voluntary to perform; observing is equal); step in, step out (de-role each time). Bridge: toward *Our Town*.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 15 - DEEPEN (Our Town - the ordinary made holy)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; read the *Our Town* material; what ordinary day would you want to live again? Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - the play's world; the ordinary made holy; gentle, not yet the death scene. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; notice one ordinary thing today as if for the last time; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep), SECOND ADULT, pre-briefed** - Emily's return: "do any human beings ever realize life while they live it?" Hold the grief that surfaces (grief is a wave - let it move through; the second adult holds any learner who needs to step out). Full de-roling; a somatic close. Bridge: carry the ordinary-is-holy into your own life.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Weeks 16-17 - APPLY (Forum Theatre - rehearse the hard call)
Each week, same shape:
- **Mon / Wed - Self-guided** - core; script a hard-call scene (yours or invented); Log; check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Forum Theatre setup (Boal): the audience can stop the scene and try a different choice; play with alternatives on your feet. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - run the hard-call scenes; the room rehearses choices bodily; de-role each time. Bridge: the choice you would actually make, and why.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 18 - HARVEST (a performed moment of empathy)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; prepare your embodiment/scene; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - dress the room; last warm-ups; confirm each learner's chosen level (perform / tribe-only / observe). *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; rest; final prep. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): HARVEST** - a performed moment of empathy - stepping fully into another life and back out whole (de-roling is part of the harvest, not an afterthought). *Being seen is a gift, never a demand.* Bridge into Session 5 (The Arena of Voices): *"You have stood in others' shoes. Now: learn from those who walk the road ahead of you."*
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 4.**

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
