# The Coach-Not-Therapist Referral Pathway

**How the guide notices, holds, and routes clinical material - without treating it**
**Status:** v0.1 - the binding gate before Session 5 runs with a real learner
**Companions:** studio-year-plan v0.3 (Part 6); The Guide's Own Floor; Session 5 day-by-day; life-coaching evidence brief

> This is the "trap door" every session's caveats point at. The guide is a life-coach and mentor, **not a licensed clinician.** Sessions surface identity work, memory, grief, and hard days by design; clinical material *will* come up. The guide's job is not to treat it - it is to **recognize it, hold the moment safely, and route it to real help, fast.** A trauma-informed floor with an unbuilt trap door is not trauma-informed. Build this before opening.

## 1. The boundary (scope of practice)

The ICF standard is the professional floor and it is clear: **a coach does not diagnose, does not treat mental-health conditions, and refers when signs indicate a learner is hampered by emotional/clinical issues beyond coaching.** ([ICF: Referring a Client to Therapy](https://coachingfederation.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/icf-research-guide-referring-client-to-therapy.pdf))

- **Coaching can:** hold space, witness, ask, support awareness, reflection, action, and accountability.
- **Coaching cannot:** diagnose, treat, counsel through trauma, or manage a mental-health crisis. Those require a licensed role the guide does not hold.
- **The move when it's past coaching:** acknowledge the limit honestly, hold the moment, and route. Routing is not failure - it is competent care.

## 2. Name the limit of confidentiality UP FRONT (Day 1)

Transparency is care (and it's the honest floor). At the start of the year - woven into the Two-Way Deal - the guide says plainly what stays private and the one thing that cannot:

> *"Almost everything you write and say here is yours and private - your Captain's Log, no one reads it to judge you. The one thing I can't keep private is if you're in danger, or someone is hurting you. Then my job is to help you get real help, and I will. That's not breaking trust - it's what care actually looks like."*

Say it before it's ever needed. A confidentiality limit disclosed in the moment feels like betrayal; disclosed up front, it's a promise kept.

## 3. Recognize: when it's past coaching (route, don't coach)

Signals that mean **route now**, not "work it in a session":
- Any mention of **suicide, self-harm, or wanting to not be here.**
- Disclosure of **abuse, neglect, or someone hurting them** (see the mandatory-report duty, §5).
- **Acute crisis** - panic, dissociation, a flashback, can't-be-reached distress.
- **Disordered eating** signals (this is why Wellness is HOLD-FOR-HUMAN).
- **Substance crisis**, or **threats to others.**
- Signs of a **mental-health condition** beyond ordinary distress (persistent, impairing).

**When in doubt, route.** You do not have to be sure. Certainty is the clinician's job, not the coach's.

## 4. The in-the-moment container (what to actually do)

- **Stay with the learner. Never leave a learner in crisis alone.**
- Move to the **designated quiet space** (name and prepare it before opening - a real room/corner, not a hallway).
- **Be present, calm, and listening. Do not fix, do not probe for details, do not counsel.** Presence, not treatment.
- **Do not promise secrecy you can't keep** (see §2).
- **The honest limit, named:** a solo guide with a worn baby has finite capacity to hold a breakdown *and* the room at once. That limit is real, not a failing. It is exactly why the whole-absence bench exists - if you cannot hold both, that is a moment to call the bench adult, a parent, or a crisis line. Naming the limit is safer than pretending it away.

## 5. The two duties you cannot coach around (Idaho)

**(a) Imminent danger to self or others.** Call **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline - call, text, or chat; 24/7; free; the Idaho Crisis & Suicide Hotline routes through 988). If life-threatening, **call 911** or go to an emergency department. ([988 Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/); [FCC 988](https://www.fcc.gov/988-suicide-and-crisis-lifeline))

**(b) Mandatory reporting.** In Idaho, **everyone is a mandatory reporter** - the guide included. Suspected **abuse, neglect, or abandonment** of a child must be reported, ideally **within 24 hours.** You do **not** need proof - report the concern and let the professionals decide. ([Idaho DHW](https://healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/services-programs/children-families-older-adults/child-and-family-services-and-foster-care-3); [RAINN Idaho summary](https://apps.rainn.org/policy/policy-state-laws-export.cfm?state=Idaho&group=4))
- **Statewide:** 855-552-KIDS (855-552-5437)
- **Treasure Valley:** 208-334-KIDS (208-334-5437)
- Or **2-1-1** (Idaho Careline), or local law enforcement.
- This duty **overrides the Log's privacy** - which is exactly why §2 names the limit on Day 1. *(Note: if the parent/guardian is the source of harm, this is a report, not a parent call - see §6.)*

## 6. Parent / guardian involvement

- For a minor's safety concern, the **parent/guardian is generally looped in**, with care for the learner's dignity and only what safety requires.
- **The exception:** where the parent/guardian *is* the concern, do **not** call them - that is a mandatory report (§5b). Route to DHW / law enforcement instead.

## 7. The referral roster (build it REAL before opening - names, not categories)

*Confirm every number/name is current before the year opens; some change.*

| Need | Resource |
|---|---|
| Suicide / crisis (self or others) | **988** (call/text/chat, 24/7); **911** if life-threatening |
| Crisis text | **Crisis Text Line** - text to **741741** |
| Idaho youth crisis center (voluntary, free, ages 5-17) | **Clarvida Youth Support Center, Boise** - 833-527-4747 (1-833-5-CRISIS) ([Idaho YES](https://yes.idaho.gov/crisis-resources/)) |
| Mandatory abuse report | 855-552-KIDS statewide / 208-334-KIDS Treasure Valley / 2-1-1 / law enforcement |
| **A named, warm-referral therapist** | **[BUILD THIS: 1-2 local licensed clinicians the studio can warm-refer to - a real name and number, arranged before opening]** |
| General info/referral | **2-1-1** Idaho Careline; SAMHSA National Helpline 800-662-4357 |

The **named therapist line is the one that must not stay blank.** "Route to real help" is only real if there is a real person to route to. Arrange it before the year opens.

## 8. After: the guide's own debrief

Holding a hard moment costs the holder. Per **The Guide's Own Floor**: after any safety event, **debrief with a supervisor or peer - do not carry it alone.** Document the incident **factually and minimally** (what happened, what you did, who you contacted) - never the learner's private content. Coach-not-therapist protects the learner; the debrief protects the guide.

## The gate (binding)

**Session 5 does not run with a real learner until:**
1. This pathway is **built** - the quiet space designated, the roster filled with **real names and current numbers** (especially the named therapist), and the Day-1 confidentiality script ready; **and**
2. The **consented Salus + Jake real-learner walk** is done.
