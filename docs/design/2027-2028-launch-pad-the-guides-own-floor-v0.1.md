# The Guide's Own Floor

**The trauma-informed floor the learners get (Session 5), applied to the guide who holds them**
**Status:** v0.1 - from Cura's "one change" in the 2026-08-23 evoked-alignment review
**Companion to:** studio-year-plan v0.3 (Part 6); all session day-by-day docs

## Why this exists

The year's deepest claim is that you can run your own life sustainably and author it without extracting from yourself. The single most powerful teaching instrument in the whole year is **the guide modeling that she does not extract from herself to serve.** A guide who runs herself into the ground to deliver the founding year would be, however lovingly, teaching the opposite of the curriculum with her own body. Teaching "run your own ship" from a ship you are sinking to keep afloat is the deepest possible incoherence.

The studio has a trauma-informed floor for the learner (Session 5: opt-out, private-by-default, name-without-detailing, coach-not-therapist). It has had **no equivalent floor for the guide.** This is that floor. It is not a concession to circumstances. It is the curriculum, applied to its teacher.

*(Written for the specific reality: Erin runs this solo, with a ~3-month-old worn, in the founding year, while also mothering and holding a household. The fleet's standing observation - that Europa extends care to everyone more completely than to herself - is exactly what this document exists to interrupt.)*

## The floor

### 1. The pre-committed pause-trigger - N = 2

If the deep Socratic block has been **pivoted away for 2 consecutive guide days** (baby, exhaustion, a hard week), the studio **pauses to fallow-mode and re-plans** rather than pushing through. Not a judgment call made while depleted - a **pre-committed rule set now, while rested.** A depleted person does not pause; that is exactly when the pause-instinct fails. N=2, not an undefined N, and not 4.

### 2. Witness-debrief - more often than monthly

Sessions 5 and 7 ask the guide to be the container for teenagers' turning points, worst days, family ruptures, grief, and self-portraits. That is real emotional labor, and it lands on the same body doing the newborn's night-holding and the household's holding. **The coach-not-therapist boundary protects the learner; it must also protect the guide.** The guide needs a real reflective/debrief structure - a supervisor, a peer, a trusted practitioner - **more often than monthly through S5 and S7**, so she has somewhere to set down what she witnesses. Monthly is not enough for the witness-load of the memory season.

### 3. Permission, in writing: pivoting is the design working

A pivoted-away deep block is not a failure of the founding cohort. A good-enough Tuesday in December is not a betrayal of the lineage. The year is called **Origin, not Mastery.** The permission the learners get - "a permanent-scaffold path is legitimate," "postpone depth, never fake it" - **the guide takes for herself.** Write it down so it is a rule, not a feeling you have to summon while tired.

### 4. The founding-cohort trap, named

"The founding cohort is the lineage - get it right" is wise about enrollment. It is a trap if it quietly raises every ordinary day to "I must get this right" in the year you have the least margin. Offer yourself the reframe you would offer a learner: **Origin is the year of claiming the whole, not shaping it perfectly.** Good-enough, sustained, beats excellent-then-collapsed.

### 5. The coach-not-therapist referral pathway - built, and it protects both

This is the trap door under the whole year, and it is currently "owed" in every session. It must be **built before Session 5 runs**, because S5 surfaces worst-days and grief by design. Built means concrete:
- **An in-the-moment crisis container:** a designated quiet space; who a crying 16-year-old actually goes to; and the honest limit that a solo guide with a worn baby has finite capacity to hold a breakdown - named, not pretended away.
- **A clinical hand-off:** the named licensed professional / route for anything past what a coach can hold.
- This pathway protects the learner *and* the guide - it is the guide's floor too, because "hold this alone" is not a plan.

## The gate

**Session 5 does not run with a real learner until (a) this referral pathway is built and (b) the consented Salus + Jake real-learner walk is done.** A trauma-informed floor with an unbuilt trap door is not yet trauma-informed. This is a gate, not a caveat.

## The one-line version

*"We evoke, we never extract" - and the person most often left off that sentence is the guide. Build her floor before the seventh session.*
