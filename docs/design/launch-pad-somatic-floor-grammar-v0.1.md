# The Launch Pad - The Movement Floor (the settle, the way-down, the exit-ramp)

**The one-page somatic grammar - the buildable form of the graduated somatic floor (P0-3)**
**Status:** v0.1 working draft - the MAC/Ring-2 deliverable (Chapel motion + McCoy soundscape, ratified by leadership v2, 2026-08-31). The floor was *named* at every scale in the spec and the year plans; this page *choreographs* it, so it can become a reflex a learner owns for life.
**Companions:** the three-year spec (Ring-1 #6 graduated floor, Ring-2 #7 crew-not-watch); the closing practice (rides this floor - "the three prompts ride on the body's return"); K'Ehleyr's settle; the coach-not-therapist referral pathway.

> **Why this exists (the finding it fixes).** "The somatic floor" was invoked in five documents and choreographed in none - "body-first settle" was a 25-minute scheduled block with nothing inside it. But the mechanism that makes a floor work across three years is *recognition*: one movement vocabulary learned so well that, by the Helm, a flooded seventeen-year-old drops into it without thinking. A settle improvised differently each guide day never becomes that reflex. **The three moves below are FIXED - same cadence, same anchor, same spoken cue, every guide day, Origin through Helm.** The sameness is not laziness; it is the medicine. Teach it in Origin S1 D2 with the closing practice's exact framing: *this is yours, you're keeping it for life.*

## The three moves

### 1. The Settle (down-regulation - opens every guide day)
A fixed, learnable sequence, not a mood.
- **The organizing principle is a lengthening exhale** (the body cannot hold sympathetic arousal on a long out-breath - this is vagal, not vibes). Three shared slow breaths, the exhale visibly longer than the inhale (roughly **4-count in, 6-count out**).
- **Guide-paced out loud, the same way every time**, the guide's own voice slowed to roughly a 60-bpm cadence. The consistency of the *words and pace* is the anchor.
- **One stable postural anchor:** "feet flat, one hand where you feel your breath." Same anchor every day.
- No device, no playlist, no soundtrack, no frequency mysticism (McCoy recanted his own 432/528Hz folklore - the drug is rhythmic co-regulation between people, not a magic number). **The anchor is the human voice, used the same way on purpose** - free, sovereign, can't glitch, and it leaves with the graduate who can say the same words to themselves at a kitchen table at 2 a.m.

### 2. The Way-Down (discharge - mid-arousal, after hard work)
The move the year plans were missing. After a deep block spikes arousal (the worst-day autobiography, the Fraud Confession, an assisted-death allegory), the body is *activated* and needs to **discharge physically** - not just "close" cognitively.
- **A standing physical reset:** stand, a shake-out, a short walk, a shared physical reset that moves the activation *through* the body before the room sits back down.
- **Seat it in the seam between the deep block and The Table** (every session). You cannot metabolize a stress spike sitting down, and digestion is parasympathetic - an activated body cannot receive a shared meal as belonging. ~3-5 minutes. It is the difference between a body that can take the meal and one white-knuckling through it.
- **Also seat it at the end of any day that ends heavy** (e.g. the Forge expedition-debrief - a body that has absorbed the world's hunger needs to come home before it drives home; do not end that day on a stakeholder map).

### 3. The Exit-Ramp (the standing offer to step out - and back)
Not just permission ("pass is always allowed") but a *path*.
- **Where the body physically goes:** a named place to step out to, established week one ("here's where you can go, and you don't have to ask").
- **A designed re-entry:** the learner slips back in during a soft block, no acknowledgment demanded, no scene. The re-entry is choreographed so returning costs nothing.
- Framed as **crew, never watch** (Ring-2 #7): the ramp is the room holding a door open, never the room watching for a fall.

## The marked quiet (McCoy - silence is a designed medium, not the absence of activity)
Unstructured silence with a self-conscious teenager is *activating*, not restful - it looks like calm and feels like exposure. Every held silence in all three years (the settle, the closing minute, the Star Chart handoff, a dissent left standing) is bounded by three parts, all things the guide already has in her body:
1. **Mark the entry** - one unchanging spoken cue, the same words and slow cadence for three years: *"We're going into the quiet now."* The body learns the cue and begins to settle before the mind catches up.
2. **Floor it** - silence only lands on an already-regulated body (the settle comes first); never use silence *as* the regulation.
3. **Bound it** - every silence has a known, cost-free way out (the exit-ramp / pass signal), so no learner is ever trapped in a silence with no door.

## Protect the guide's own nervous system (McCoy + Vic - the single point of failure)
Co-regulation is a *physiological output*: the guide spends her own vagal tone to settle a flooded learner, all day, solo, with an infant worn on her body. **The guide runs her own settle, her own way-down at day's end, and her own closing minute - as safety instrumentation, not self-care** - printed on the one-page-per-season Operating Page she carries. Her regulation is on the page, or it is not in the design. (See leadership v2, the guide-in-series finding; and P0-2, the guide-sustainability watch + honorable-defer blessing.)

## What is fixed vs. free
- **Fixed (never improvised):** the settle's 4-in/6-out cadence and its one postural anchor; the way-down as a standing physical discharge in the deep-block→meal seam; the exit-ramp's named place and no-scene re-entry; the marked-quiet's spoken entry cue. Same, Origin through Helm.
- **Free (guide's judgment in the room):** which day needs the way-down at the end (read the room); how long a held quiet runs; the exact walk or shake-out. The *shape* is fixed; the *dosing* is the guide's craft.

*"We evoke - we never extract." - the most anti-extractive thing here is a regulatory move a young person owns for life, that needs no guide and no app. Give them the same pause, three years running, and they walk out able to come home to themselves. That is motion as medicine. That is the point.*
