# Launch Pad Year 3 (The Helm) - Session 5 "The Arena of Voices" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.2 working draft (fresh-eyes elevation) | **When:** weeks 19-21 (short session; mentor infrastructure precedes it - see below)
**Companions:** Year 3 studio-year plan (Helm); Helm Sessions 1-4; three-year spec (Season Three)

## The session at a glance
- **Arc:** OPEN (the art of being mentored) -> DEEPEN (real professional mentors + Boise guest speakers) -> HARVEST (a real conversation with someone walking the road ahead - and what it opened).
- **The thread:** *no one navigates alone. The people further down your road are a gift - and being mentored well is a skill: come prepared, ask a question worth a busy person's time, follow up. A good question is the reciprocal gift.*
- **The work:** the art of being mentored (prepare, ask, follow up); the informational interview; a pitch (what you are building / where you are headed); Boise guest speakers who have made real hard calls.
- **Helm rhythm:** Tue guided-lighter (interview craft, pitch practice, guest speakers - toddler-flexible), Thu guided-deep (reflection on what the voices opened). Mon/Wed self-guided; Fri fallow. The Vineyard runs alongside.
- **"Learning the terrain from those who walked it" (Kirk)** - name S5's place in the year: the mentors and guest speakers are how the captain learns the ground before the Council and the Launch.

## The two-track design (BINDING - see the operating plan)
- **Guest speakers (the window):** guide present the whole visit (satisfies never-alone); public/observable setting; a short expectations sheet the speaker acknowledges; parent notification of who and when. **No background check** for a one-time supervised group speaker - the supervision is the control. Watch the conversion point: any speaker who wants to keep contact with a learner routes follow-up through the guide (which moves them to the mentor track).
- **1:1 professional mentors (the door):** NOT cleared until the mentor infrastructure exists - observable channel only (no private DMs/social/off-platform); default remote-and-monitored, in-person only with the guide present or a public setting; **background check** before first contact; three-party informed consent (parent, learner, mentor) + signed boundaries; a no-fault off-ramp (one word to the guide ends a match, no reason owed); a written safeguarding + mandatory-reporting policy; and **counsel review** (Idaho duty-of-care + liability/insurance). Build the container before the first match.
- **Reciprocity (Kasidy):** pay real honoraria + costs (a waived fee only if freely offered); a menu of depths (one interview / a short arc / a sustained 1:1) with the one-off honored identically to the year-long; bounded terms + dignified exit; the teen arrives prepared; close the loop (thanks, tell them what their gift did, ask if it served them); **the story is a gift, never the price** (a hard call - especially a trauma-proximate one - is invited, never required as content); continuity over churn; the mentor is not the load-bearing safeguarding adult.

## Care points
- **Mentor-rejection preload (Jake):** reaching out to an admired professional and being ignored is a real wound at this age. Teach *before* the outreach: "a non-response is not a verdict on you - busy people are busy." Preloaded, not consoled after.
- Sourcing is reciprocal and warm - through communities that already hold relationship (the VL parent network, Boise professional/civic bodies, field-specific reach shaped by the learners' own interests), never cold extraction. George costs and does outreach; Kira/Stirps if it touches refugee/immigrant communities.
- Carry the somatic settle and glad-close.
- Coach-not-therapist carries; keep the mentor boundary bright.

## Week 19 - OPEN (the art of being mentored)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core / HS credits; who is walking the road you want to walk? Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - the art of being mentored (prepare, ask a real question, follow up); the mentor-rejection preload; draft your interview questions. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; research your field/person; write a question worth a busy person's time; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - why we need voices ahead of us; what a good question gives the person answering it; the pitch shaped. Bridge: prepare for a real conversation.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 20 - DEEPEN (the voices arrive)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; final prep for your informational interview / mentor conversation; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter) - GUEST SPEAKER(S)** - a Boise professional who has made real hard calls (guide present; expectations sheet acknowledged; reciprocal - they get to ask the learners too; fed at the Table). *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; conduct your informational interview (observable channel; prepared); Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - reflect on the voices: what surprised you? What did a real hard-call story teach that a lesson could not? Bridge: what will you carry?
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 21 - HARVEST (a real conversation, and what it opened)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; write up what your mentor/interview opened; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - share what the voices gave; thank the givers (notes the learners write); close the loop. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; one next step your conversation pointed to; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): HARVEST** - a real conversation with someone ahead of you, and the honest account of what it opened (or did not). *Empty-basket honored: "the interview did not land, and here is what I learned about reaching out" is real.* Bridge into Session 6 (The Bridge): *"You have learned from those who lead. Now: practice leading - and being led."*
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 5.**

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
