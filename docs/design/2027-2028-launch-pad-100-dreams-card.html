100 Dreams

The dream session - after Matthew Kelly, The Dream Manager
Write 100 dreams. No filter. No ceiling. Nothing has to be realistic. Don't edit a single one.
The wall at 40. Somewhere around dream 40 the easy ones run out and you'll feel stuck. That is exactly where the real ones start - so keep going past it. The best dreams live on the far side of the wall.
