# The Site Walk - a sheet to carry

**For:** the Launch Pad studio room, before the access statement publishes.
**Fills:** the `[PENDING SITE WALK]` section of `launch-pad-access-statement-v0.1-DRAFT.md`, and picks the designated quiet space that `2027-2028-launch-pad-coach-not-therapist-referral-pathway-v0.1.md` has owed since it was written.
**Takes:** about half an hour.
**Status:** v0.1, 2026-09-28.

---

## Before you go

**Bring:** a tape measure, your phone, this sheet, a pen.

**The one tip that finds the most in the least time:** push the pram through the whole route, from where a family parks to the chair you would sit in. A pram is not a wheelchair, but it finds thresholds, narrow doors, tight corners and heavy doors in about four minutes, and your hands are full the same way.

**The rule this walk runs under.** This is not a compliance audit and nobody is grading the building. The job is to write down what is true so the statement can say it plainly. A family reading "there is one step at the front door, and here is how we get around it" can decide for themselves. A family reading nothing has to guess, and the ones who guess wrong just never come back.

**The reference numbers below are the common accessible minimums,** given so a measurement means something. They are reference points, not a verdict. Whether any of them legally bind a private program in Idaho is a question for counsel, and it rides with the bundle already going there.

**The third column is the important one.** Plenty of what you find will be cheap to change: move a table, prop a door, add a lamp, swap a chair. Knowing which is the difference between "we cannot" and "we have not yet."

## After the walk

1. Fill *The space* in the access statement from the third column, in plain sentences. Name the step, name the workaround.
2. Name the quiet space in the referral pathway, and list what it needs.
3. Anything in the "fixable" column that costs under an afternoon: do it, and the statement describes the fixed version.
4. Anything expensive: it goes in the statement as it is, honestly.
5. Send the photos and this sheet to Chekov for the Tier 1 read.
