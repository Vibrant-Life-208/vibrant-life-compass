# Launch Pad Year 3 (The Helm) - Session 3 "The Ripple" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.2 working draft (fresh-eyes elevation) | **When:** weeks 10-12 (short session; a lighter beat after S2)
**Companions:** Year 3 studio-year plan (Helm); Helm Sessions 1-2; three-year spec (Season Three)

## The session at a glance
- **Arc:** OPEN (every choice sends ripples) -> DEEPEN (2nd- and 3rd-order effects; the Pre-Mortem) -> HARVEST (a decision traced forward, ripples named).
- **The thread:** *a hard call does not end when you make it. It sets things in motion - some you intend, some you do not. Consequence-thinking is looking one, two, three moves downstream before you act - and being honest that you cannot see all the way.*
- **The tools:** the Ripple Board (map the downstream effects of a choice), the Monkey's Paw (the wish granted with a twist - unintended consequences as story), counterfactual history (what if this one thing had gone differently?), the Pre-Mortem (imagine the choice failed - why?).
- **Helm rhythm:** Tue guided-lighter (what-if games, the Ripple Board, the Monkey's Paw - toddler-flexible), Thu guided-deep (the Pre-Mortem and second/third-order reasoning). Mon/Wed self-guided; Fri fallow.
- **Deliberately lighter and playful** - a breath between the two heaviest sessions (S2 and S4). Safe by design; no second adult needed.
- **The Vineyard runs alongside** (Guinan) - the standing weekly joy-for-its-own-sake thread; this lighter session is a natural place to let it breathe.

## Care points
- This session is light and playful on purpose - it lets the room exhale after S2 and before S4. Keep it that way; do not import heavy material.
- Consequence-thinking can tip into anxiety (paralysis by "what if everything goes wrong") - always pair it with agency ("and here is the move you can still make"); the point is wiser action, not fear of acting.
- Carry the somatic open-settle and glad-close; a light session still bookends.
- Coach-not-therapist carries.

## Week 10 - OPEN (every choice sends ripples)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core / HS credits; a small choice you made that had effects you did not expect; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - what-if games; the Monkey's Paw (the wish with a twist); every choice sends ripples. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; map the ripples of one recent decision; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - the Ripple Board: 1st -> 2nd -> 3rd-order effects of a choice; where the intended and the unintended diverge. Bridge: pick a real decision to trace.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 11 - DEEPEN (looking downstream)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; trace your chosen decision two and three moves out; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - counterfactual history - change one thing, follow the ripples; play with the fragility of "how things turned out." *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; who is affected downstream that you had not considered? Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - the Pre-Mortem: imagine your choice failed - why? What did the ripples do? Turn foresight into a better plan. Bridge: what would you change now?
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 12 - HARVEST (a decision traced forward)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; finalize your traced decision (ripples, blind spots, the still-available move); Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - share ripple-maps; the room adds effects each learner missed - consequence-thinking as a team sport. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; a Log entry on what consequence-thinking changed for you. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): HARVEST** - a decision traced forward, ripples named, blind spots owned, agency kept. *Empty-basket honored: "the honest finding is I cannot see far enough - so here is how I will stay humble and adjust" is real.* Bridge into Session 4 (The Stage): *"You can reason a call and trace it. Now: step into someone else's shoes and feel it."*
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 3.**

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
