# Launch Pad Year 3 (The Helm) - Session 2 "No Easy Answers" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft | **When:** weeks 4-9 (long session, 6 weeks)
**Companions:** Year 3 studio-year plan (Helm); Helm Session 1; three-year spec (Season Three); coach-not-therapist referral pathway

## The session at a glance
- **Arc:** OPEN (a dilemma has no answer key) -> DEEPEN (real moral dilemmas through allegory; Kohlberg's stages) -> APPLY (reason a dilemma through, out loud, from principle) -> HARVEST (a reasoned position on a hard case - held, not "won").
- **The thread:** *the world does not hand you the answer key. The win is not the right answer; it is the quality of the reasoning and the honesty about the cost. You learn to stand inside a dilemma long enough to reason from principle rather than reach for the exit.*
- **The method:** moral dilemmas through TNG allegory tied to real cases - Measure of a Man / Dred Scott (personhood); The Drumhead / McCarthyism (due process under fear); The First Duty / whistleblowing (loyalty vs truth). Kohlberg's stages named as a map, not a ladder to climb for a grade.
- **Helm rhythm:** Tue guided-lighter (dilemma games, the reasoning craft, Kohlberg map - toddler-flexible), Thu guided-deep (the heavy dilemma work). Mon/Wed self-guided; Fri fallow.

## Care points (this is one of the three heaviest sessions)
- **SECOND ADULT REQUIRED on the heaviest deep days** (the assisted-death, coercion, and identity-conformity episodes) - not childcare; a second pair of eyes whose only job is to leave with the learner who needs to leave, so the guide never chooses between the room and the wound. Where a second adult cannot be secured for a given heavy week, that deep block lightens or defers.
- **Allegory is the safety technology, not the watered-down version.** Data's trial lets a room reason about personhood and slavery at full weight while each learner's own unhealed places stay behind the glass. Keep the allegory; it is what makes maximum honesty survivable.
- **Preview-and-alternate the heaviest episodes** - *Ethics* (assisted death), *Chain of Command* (coercion/torture), *The Outcast* (identity/conformity). Alternates pre-selected before the day, not improvised. Name the terrain and the exit in the pre-brief.
- **Honest about the world, only-as-deep-as-chosen about the self.** The dilemma can be unflinching; the learner's own interior is never required as material. Dilemmas resolve into *reasoning*, never "the right answer" - the most honest choice, because it tells the truth about the world they are entering.
- **Somatic floor:** open settle -> mid-work discharge points (30-sec orienting between activations) -> close return, every heavy day. Pre-brief before every flagged episode (arrival, not ambush). Battle-buddy pairing and a physical exit ramp in place before the heavy material opens.
- Coach-not-therapist carries; the referral pathway is live.

## Week 4 - OPEN (a dilemma has no answer key)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core / HS credits; a real decision you have faced with no clean right answer - what made it hard? Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - what a moral dilemma is (vs a problem with a solution); the reasoning craft; Kohlberg's stages as a map of *how* people reason, not a rank. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; watch/read the week's allegory case; note where you land and why. Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - Measure of a Man / personhood (Dred Scott alongside): who counts as a person, and who decides? Reason from principle. Bridge: whose reasoning changed yours?
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 5 - DEEPEN (due process under fear)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; the week's case; where does fear distort judgment? Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - dilemma games; how a good process protects a hard call; steelmanning the side you disagree with. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; a time fear shaped a group's decision (allegorical or historical); Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - The Drumhead / McCarthyism: due process when a room is afraid. Reason from principle. Bridge: what protects the accused when everyone is certain?
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 6 - DEEPEN (loyalty vs truth)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; the week's case; when is loyalty a virtue and when a trap? Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - the reasoning craft continues; the cost of every choice named honestly. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; a hard call between loyalty and truth; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - The First Duty / whistleblowing: the first duty is to the truth. Reason from principle; name what it costs. Bridge: toward the heaviest cases, previewed.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Weeks 7-8 - APPLY (the heaviest cases - previewed, alternated, second adult)
Each week, same shape - **second adult present on the deep day:**
- **Mon / Wed - Self-guided** - core; the previewed case (alternate available, no reason owed); reason it through in the Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - the reasoning craft; process the *reasoning*, not personal disclosure. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Thu - Guided (deep), pre-briefed** - the heaviest episodes across the two weeks (*Ethics* / assisted death; *Chain of Command* / coercion; *The Outcast* / identity-conformity), each with its alternate. Allegory-first; opt-out with no reason owed; reason from principle. Bridge: hold the tension without resolving it.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 9 - HARVEST (a reasoned position, held not won)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; choose a dilemma; write your reasoned position (principle, cost, dissent honored); Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - share reasoning (not verdicts); the room practices honoring a well-reasoned position it disagrees with. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; refine your position; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): HARVEST** - a reasoned position on a hard case, held from principle, not "won." *Empty-basket honored: "I reasoned my way to 'I still do not know' - and here is why" is a real and honest harvest.* Bridge into Session 3 (The Ripple): *"You can reason a hard call. Now: trace what it sets in motion."*
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 2.**

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
