# Launch Pad Year 1 - Session 1 "The Map of Me" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft
**Dates:** Aug 16 - Sep 3, 2027 (weeks 1-3 of Origin)
**Companions:** `2027-2028-launch-pad-studio-year-plan-v0.3.md` (the operating plan) and `2026-06-24-launch-pad-three-year-story-cycle-v0.1.md` (the three-year design)

## The session at a glance

- **Arc:** OPEN (dream it all) → DEEPEN (sort + map) → APPLY (courage) → HARVEST (choose 3 goals to carry)
- **The thread underneath:** *your life is bigger than you've let yourself imagine, and you can start naming it.*
- **Source:** the 100 Dreams practice is Matthew Kelly, *The Dream Manager* (2007) - 100 personal dreams, three time-horizons, shared in a "dream session" where dreams are contagious.
- **The map (fleet decision 2026-08-21):** dreams sort into the **five Compass regions** the learner already navigates - **Self / Others / Making / World / Voice** - with warm dream-prompts nested in each (Kelly's evocative words, teen-relabeled). One coherent map, not a competing twelve-taxonomy. See the decision log.
- **Runs through every day:** the Captain's Log (named Day 1, added to daily); core academics build in weeks 2-3; the credit/path-map audit happens weeks 2-3 (after belonging is rooted, never Day 2 morning); accountability partners; the Monday re-entry ritual.
- **Guide-day structure:** Tue/Thu 9:00-1:00, baby worn, the one deep block ~10:00-11:00 (fed before, timed to the nap, pivot ready). Full structure in studio-plan v0.3, Part 2.

### The five-region dream-prompt map (from the fleet decision)

| Region (Compass) | Warm dream-prompts nested inside (teen-relabeled Kelly) |
|---|---|

*Dreaming is free and unfiltered first (Day 1-2); sorting into the regions comes later (Day 9). The prompts are doorways, not filing rules.*

The shared-meal belonging ritual on every guide day (from the "Live & Love Life" thread). The guide sits and eats the same food; the base meal is always provided (no cover charge); allergen/cultural/ED-safe; presence, not consumption (no one watches a plate). The worn baby sits at the center. Open sub-question: 11:00-11:30 inside the guide day vs. the 1:00 lunch.

## Week 1 (Aug 16-20) - OPEN

### Day 1 · Mon Aug 16 · Orientation (12:00-2:00, special late start)
Belonging first. Walk-in & The Table → Opening Circle (first "how are you, really?" + name round; "pass is always allowed") → The Map of the Year → The Two-Way Deal (six refusals as a promise, then co-write the tribe's agreements; contributions may be written on a card, not only spoken) → What Origin is + **name your Captain's Log** + a 2-minute first entry → The Constellation (tribe-building) → **The First Quest + Glad Close.**

**The send-off (the assignment):** *"Your first quest, before Tuesday: write 100 dreams. No filter, no ceiling, nothing has to be realistic, don't edit a single one. You'll hit a wall around 40 where the easy ones run out - that's exactly where the real ones start, so keep going. Bring your list Tuesday and we'll dig into them together."*

**Take-home 100 Dreams card:** the rule (100, unfiltered, unrealistic-allowed, no editing) · the wall at 40 · a spark bank of warm words (places to go, things to make, people to meet, who to become, what to give, adventures, what you'd be brave enough to try, an ordinary day you'd love to live - Body, Heart, Family, Friends, Love, Money & Freedom, Craft, Learning, Adventure, Soul, Legacy) · "steal any dream that lights you up - borrowed dreams count" · "what did you want when you were eight?" · however they come out is fine (written, voice-recorded, drawn). *100 is the target, not a gate - bring what you've got.*

### Day 2 · Tue Aug 17 · Guide-led (9:00-1:00) - The Dream Session
They arrive *with* their dreams. Baby worn.

| Time | Block |

| 11:00-11:30 | The Table - baby worn at the center |
| 11:30-12:30 | Apply & Collaborate - keep building toward 100 in company; **teach the deep-work block** (first solo one is tomorrow) |
| 12:30-1:00 | Bridge + Glad Close - Wednesday plan (finish 100; start noticing which region each dream lives in); re-entry note |

**Inside the Dream Session (Kelly's heart):**
- **Debrief first** - "Who hit the wall at 40? What surfaced after?" Makes the wall a shared story, not a private failure.
- **Go around the circle** - each shares one dream aloud (start with a within-12-months one, per Kelly).
- **Dreams are contagious** - Kelly's rule: *"if you hear a dream you want, reach for your pen and add it to your list."* Watch the lists grow live - tribe-building and dream-expansion in one move.
- **The guide begins to hold each learner's dreams** - the dream-manager relationship, seeded day one: as they share, you find yourself thinking *"how could we help you live that one?"* This is why the guide is a life-coach, not a teacher - engage the learner in their own life first.
- **A non-verbal door** - a shared "tribe dreams" wall for the shy.
- **In-room catch-up** - anyone who stalled solo gets warm prompts + a partner; nobody who came with 20 leaves ashamed.

### Day 9 · Thu Aug 26 · Guide-led - map across the five regions
Work of the Day: map the dreams across the **five Compass regions** (Self / Others / Making / World / Voice), using the warm nested prompts to make sure no part of life gets missed. Notice which regions are *full* and which are *quiet* - **the quiet regions are the invitation, not a lack** ("a thin region is a door you haven't walked through yet"). *"What would a fuller [quiet region] even look like?"* Because it's the same five regions they navigate all year, the dream-map becomes their Compass at higher resolution - not a foreign taxonomy. **Bridge:** Monday - sketch into the thin regions; re-entry note.

## Week 3 (Aug 30 - Sep 3) - APPLY → HARVEST

### Day 11 · Mon Aug 30 · Self-guided

Work of the Day: *"Which of these scares you a little? Courage points there."* Then Covey's created-twice - to learners: *you're dreaming it first; the next years are where you build it.* The far-horizon dreams are the "end to begin with." **Bridge:** Wednesday - narrow toward the within-12-months dreams you'd actually carry; re-entry note.

### Day 13 · Wed Sep 1 · Self-guided
Re-entry. Choose candidate within-12-months dreams; core; Log; come ready Thursday to name three.

### Day 14 · Thu Sep 2 · Guide-led - HARVEST: choose 3 season goals + bridge to The Lineage
Work of the Day: each learner picks **3 within-12-months dreams** as the season's orienting goals (these become the proximal goals that feed later WOOP work) - **any goal can be changed, dropped, or outgrown at any checkpoint with no failure attached; the dream you no longer want is data about who you're becoming, not a missed target.** A small voluntary share - the map made real, and *"I have nothing to declare yet" is a true and honored harvest.* Close Session 1. **Bridge into Session 2 (The Lineage):** *"Start thinking about where you come from - the people who made you. Not just blood; anyone who left a mark."*

**The Closing Feast** (session-close ritual - see `2027-2028-launch-pad-closing-feast-boise-tables-v0.2.md`): the tribe closes the session by sharing a neighbor's table - a family who cooks near us - honoring the cook and their craft (never anyone's journey unless freely offered), reciprocal (pay real custom; return-patronage), inclusion-floored, and built to be *returned to*, not toured. **Binding gate:** the Kira/Stirps cultural-respect sign-off + a real owner/broker in the room before the first feast. S1's is the tribe's first table; the year widens the circle of "us."
