# Launch Pad Year 1 - Session 1 "The Map of Me" - Materials List

**Status:** v0.1 - prep/shopping checklist for the first three weeks (Aug 16 - Sep 3, 2027)
**Companions:** Session 1 day-by-day; studio-year-plan v0.3 (handout appendices); Closing Feast v0.2

*Cohort size drives quantities - buy for your real number (4-7), plus a couple of spares.*

## Per-learner kit (one each)

- **Captain's Log** - a real, good notebook the learner names and owns (carried all three years, so buy one worth keeping). Day 1.
- **Weekly Plan pad** - printed from Appendix A (accessible layout: starter banks, not blank lines). Used every self-guided day.
- **Credit / Path Map** - printed from Appendix B (the "Remaining" column co-computed or shown as a bar, never left as unaided subtraction). Weeks 2-3.
- **Take-home 100 Dreams card** - the rule, the wall-at-40 note, the warm spark bank. Handed out Day 1.
- **Pens / pencils** they like (a small thing that matters for a writing-heavy season).

## Shared studio materials (reusable across the year)

- **A wall or large board** - for the tribe's co-written agreements (Two-Way Deal, Day 1) and the growing "tribe dreams" wall (Dream Session, Day 2).
- **Sticky notes - lots** (the dreams wall, the horizon sort, the region map). Multiple colors help.
- **Markers, and dot-stickers or highlighters** (the courage pass, Day 12).
- **The Year-Map visual** - one page, printed big: the three seasons, the weekly rhythm, the harvests. Day 1.
- **The Five-Region Dream Map** - a poster or table-mat of the Compass regions (Self / Others / Making / World / Voice) with the warm nested prompts (Body/Heart/Family/Friends/Love/Money & Freedom/Craft/Things I Want/Learning/Adventure/Soul/Legacy). Used Day 9; leave it up.
- **Floor markers / painter's tape** - for the Constellation, so it reads "**place yourself**" (a spot to move to), never "stand" - every kinetic activity gets an equal seated/low-mobility path.
- **A gentle chime or timer** - optional, for teaching the deep-work block (Day 2) without a harsh classroom bell.
- **Question-cards** for the Day 1 walk-in.

## The Table (every guide day - Tue/Thu)

- **Food - always provided, always enough** (the inclusion floor: no learner ever pays-to-participate). Plan allergen/dietary/religious-safe options in advance.
- Plates, utensils, napkins, cups. Reusable if you can.
- *A note, not a material: presence not consumption - no one's plate is watched.*

## Reading & core academics

- **Books** - lean on the **VL lending library** (the checkout system already set up) so reading materials cost nothing and rotate.
- **Learner-specific academic materials** - each learner brings/needs their own core-academics resources (texts, workbooks, laptop) per their Credit/Path Map. Confirm per learner in the week-1-2 credit audit.

## Accessibility must-haves (Chekov's conditions, as materials)

- **Handouts in accessible format** - left-aligned (never justified) sans-serif, generous line spacing, high contrast (4.5:1 on any color); the Stuck Protocol posted large on the wall in the same accessible layout.
- **Starter-bank versions** of the Weekly Plan (checkbox priorities to select-then-customize, not a blank page).
- **An icon/visual variant** of the Weekly Plan for a learner who freezes at a text form.
- **A voice-record / draw option** for the 100 Dreams (however the dreams come out is fine).
- **The seated/low-mobility path** for the Constellation and any "place yourself" activity.
