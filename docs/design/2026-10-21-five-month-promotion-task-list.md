# The Launch Pad - five month task list, Erin and Jenna

**Written 21 October 2026. Window: now through Friday 19 March 2027.**
**Goal: seven learners with signed Family notes by 19 March.**

Every line has one owner and one date. If a line has no owner it does not happen.

---

## Read this part first, because two things in it change the plan

### 1. Nobody has counted the band

The whole invitation rests on "current Vibrant Life families whose learner turns 16 by the 2027 cutoff." That number is written down nowhere. Not in the year plan, not in the promotion plan, not in the status page.

Seven learners out of a band of nine is a different job from seven out of twenty-five. One is a near-sweep of every eligible family in the school, which is not realistic and should not be planned for. The other is comfortable.

**This is the first task on the list and it takes an afternoon.** Everything below is provisional until it is answered.

Pre-decide the branch now, while nobody is anxious:

| Families in the band | What happens |
|---|---|
| 20 or more | Circles 1 and 2 only. Hold circles 3 and 4 in reserve. |
| 12 to 19 | Circles 1 and 2 first, circle 3 opens in December rather than March. |
| Under 12 | Circles 1 through 4 all open in December, and seven is probably the wrong target for year one. Five is a real cohort. |

Deciding this in October costs nothing. Deciding it in February, with four chairs filled, is when a paid advert starts to look like rescue.

### 2. Seven by 19 March means the admissions clock moves forward about five weeks

The existing timeline puts letters in January, the evening in February, conversations through February and March, the learners' own yeses in April and countersigned notes in May. On that clock, seven signed notes land in May, not March.

There is already a recommendation on record that the admissions clock should move rather than the leave plan. **Moving it forward is the version that works.** It hits the target and it clears the spring birth window completely, instead of leaving the three most delicate moments of the whole arc, the learner's solo yes and the two hard reply letters, sitting inside it.

So everything below runs about five weeks earlier than the old plan. The cost is that November and early December are heavy. The benefit is that March is finished and April is free.

One thing that does not move, at any price. A learner is asked for their own yes **alone, about a week after** their conversation, with no parent in the room. If the calendar gets tight, the calendar moves. That week does not compress.

## What we do not do, for the whole five months

- No paid advertising, boosting, or any form of bought reach.
- No Launch Pad account or page on any platform. If anything is said socially it is Vibrant Life's own account, in Jenna and Wes's words.
- No event page for the evening. It publishes a guest list of families privately deciding about their children.
- No list built from families, no tracking of who opened or clicked anything, no QR code that identifies anyone.
- No second letter and no follow-up to silence.
- No photo, name, story, work or voice of any learner, including the pilot, including with a parent's signature.
- No number about a learner, ever, including how many families came to the evening.
- Nothing from the pilot morning is shown anywhere.
- The seat count is a fact when a family asks and a lever when it leads. It never opens anything.

The two numbers worth keeping: conversations held, and chairs filled. Nothing else.

If the answer is a document, name that and redirect. No shame in it. The first four months of this project produced a great deal of excellent paper and not one morning in a room, and the only thing that fixes is a date on a calendar.
