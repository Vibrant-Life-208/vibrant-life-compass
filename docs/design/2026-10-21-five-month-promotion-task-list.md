# The Launch Pad - five month plan, Erin and Jenna

**Rewritten 22 October 2026. Window: now through Friday 19 March 2027.**
**Goal: seven learners with signed Family notes by 19 March.**

Four phases. Be known, be findable, be visited, then the door. Every line has one owner and one date.

---

## What changed, and why this is a rewrite rather than an edit

**The band is one family.** The old plan assumed the room would fill from current Vibrant Life families with a learner turning sixteen. There is one. Six of the seven have to come from the wider Caldwell and Canyon County homeschool world, which is relationship work measured in months, not a list you write to. Those circles were held in reserve. They are now the main channel and they open in November.

**No pilot morning.** The first cohort starts fresh on 16 August 2027. That removes the one place a real sixteen-year-old could have changed the design before a family committed, which means the open evenings in January become the first time anyone outside the room meets this. It also means the door document, which currently says "not before the pilot," has to be rewritten rather than left contradicting itself.

**Jenna is the second adult.** That closes a chair that has been filled by a document. It needs one paragraph in writing, including what she does when being the employer and the price-setter conflicts with being the person a family can safely say no to.

**We are in Caldwell, not Boise.** Every family-facing artifact currently says Boise, including both new posters now in the shared folder. Caldwell is a different city in a different county about twenty-five miles away, and a Caldwell family does not call itself a Boise family. This is a one-word fix on five files and it should happen before anything is printed.

### The honest arithmetic

Seven learners aged sixteen to eighteen, in Canyon County, from relationship networks, starting from one known family, inside five months, is a hard number. Plan for seven. Do not treat four as a failure. The cycle lets a learner join any year and the Ballast exists for exactly that, so a first cohort of four is a real cohort and not a compromise.

**One rule across all four phases: the limits travel with the warmth.** Every artifact in phases one and two carries the price, the one guide, the no aide, the baby in the room, and the record no college yet recognizes. A family should be able to decide against us from a cork board. That is what keeps this from being a campaign.

## What we do not do, for the whole five months

- No paid advertising, boosting, or any bought reach.
- No Launch Pad account or page on any platform. Anything said socially is Jenna's own account in her own words.
- No event page for any evening. It publishes a guest list of families privately deciding about their children.
- No list built from families, no tracking of who opened or clicked anything, no QR code that identifies anyone.
- No application or submission form. A family says they want to talk, and that is the whole mechanism. An application implies selection, selection implies a bar, and you will be under-subscribed rather than over-subscribed.
- No second letter and no follow-up to silence.
- No photo, name, story, work or voice of any learner, ever, including with a parent's signature.
- No number about a learner, including how many families came to an evening.
- The seat count is a fact when a family asks and a lever when it leads. It never opens anything.

The two numbers worth keeping: conversations held, and chairs filled.

## The Friday question

Every Friday, out loud, between the two of you.

**"Who met us this week who had not met us before?"**

In phases one and two that is the only question that matters. If the answer is nobody, the week did not move, however much got made.
