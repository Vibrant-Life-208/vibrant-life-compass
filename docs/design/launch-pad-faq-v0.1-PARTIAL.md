### Will this count for high school?

Your core academic work is yours to claim and yours to pace, and you do it on the two mornings you run yourselves. **High-school credit is tracked separately from everything else here.** It is the one record other people read as a routine matter, which is the honest exception to the first promise we make about your progress being yours.

### Will a college accept it?

Here is the whole truth and it is uncomfortable. **A transcript from a studio like this one is not yet a thing every college recognises.** The bridge to dual enrollment is real work, and we will do it alongside you rather than pretend it is already finished.

What that means in practice: your credit record exists and travels. The fuller record of what you actually did here is a different thing, and the part that lets it physically leave as an export you hold in your hands **is not built yet.** Until it is, nobody walks the Launch at the end of the three years, because a ceremony that hands you something that is not there is not a ceremony.

### What does my learner actually do on the mornings nobody is teaching?

Their reading, their core academics at their own pace, their personal project, and the session's writing. The guide is in the building, at Vibrant Life Tots with the younger children from 9:15 to 11:30, and comes to them around noon for a real check-in. Same site, different room. If they need the guide before noon they go and get them.

They are genuinely running their own hours, and they are never alone in the building. Both of those are true at once, and we would rather say both than only the comfortable one.

### What gets written down about my learner, and who reads it?

The Captain's Log is theirs and nobody reads it, including the guide and including you. Growth is only ever shown as movement compared to their own earlier self: never a grade, never a score, never set beside another learner. A parent linked to the account can see which of their learner's own goals are finished, and that is the whole of what anyone else sees.

The agreements record is the exception, and only if a Stop is ever reached. A Stop note goes home the same day and your learner fills it in with the guide. The count clears at the start of each year, and none of it goes on a transcript or travels with the Growth Record.
