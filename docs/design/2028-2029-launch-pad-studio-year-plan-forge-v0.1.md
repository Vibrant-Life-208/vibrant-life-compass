> Successor in spirit to the Year-1 studio plan (v0.3). The machine is the same; the content is The Forge; two things are corrected for Year 2 - the toddler is now mobile (not a worn napper), and the cohort now has returners + joiners.

## The two decisions this plan is built on (from the upstream panel)

1. **The Forge is not solo-runnable at full intensity with a mobile toddler** (unanimous). The Year-1 design rested on "a worn 3-month-old naps to the motion of a teacher" - that fact expires the day the baby walks. **Chosen shape: the lighter Forge** - one deep Socratic day per week, one lighter guided day, named to families as the founder's season. (The alternative - full two-deep-day Forge - would require secured toddler care ~$5-7k/yr; deferred in favor of the sustainable solo shape.)
2. **One cohort, one season at a time; the founding cohort moves together into The Forge; new 16-18s join in progress** (unanimous). Running simultaneous seasons solo is not operable. But "join in progress, never late" must be **built, not asserted** - see the joiner scaffold and returner reach-back below.

## What The Forge is

*Lens: what is worth solving? Joy-flavor: curiosity & company. Anchor: "Things are only impossible until they're not." - Picard*

Where Origin was **dreaming it** (Covey's first creation - "who am I?"), The Forge is **building it** (the second creation). It is also **the window** the Year-1 evoked review said Origin lacked: the whole year turns the gaze outward, from the self to the world and its problems. Self (Y1) -> world (Y2) -> the hard call (Y3).

**Signature daily ritual:** the **Impossible Question** (replaces Year 1's choice games) - a question with no clean answer, debated playfully, to grow comfortable standing inside something too big to close.

*The lighter day (Tue) is where a mobile toddler fits fine - it's the same baby-flexible content Year 1 kept around its deep block. The deep day (Thu) is the one hour that needs the toddler managed.*

## The toddler-year model (the one deep hour)

The whole toddler collision is now contained to **one morning's deep block (Thu ~10:00-11:00)**. Handle it three ways, in order:
1. **Light, single-morning coverage** - one adult for that one hour: a grandparent, Stephen on a light day, one rotating parent, the extended Tots-side adult, or the toddler off-site just Thursdays. *One* morning is findable informally where two required a paid line. This is a light precondition, not a $5-7k gate.
2. **The split-circle + pivot discipline** - a once-weekly deep block that has to pivot away (toddler day) is recoverable: resume next week. A single weekly deep hour is far more forgiving than two.
3. **Structure the hour tight** - fed/settled before, kept to the hour, the room's maturity holding a short pause (incidentally, never as childcare).

**Named to families:** The Forge runs at one-deep-day intensity this year because it is the founder's toddler season. Honest, not hidden - the depth cut is real and owned, not a quiet degradation.

**December is harder this year** (~18 months: molars, the "no" phase, separation-anxiety peak). Pre-commit the soft landing now, while rested.

### The Guide's Own Floor, amended for Year 2 (Cura)

The Year-1 floor was built for *episodic* depletion; the toddler is *structural*. So:
- **A weekly presence-check** (not only the N=2 absence-trigger): "was I whole for the deep hour this week, or did I bail water?" Structural drain needs a structural detector.
- **The witness-debrief is now load-bearing** - a named person and cadence before the memory-adjacent work, not "even a monthly call."
- **Permission extended to the program's shape** - the lighter Forge *is* the floor working; a smaller cohort or a further-lightened season is the design honoring the guide, not the guide failing.
- **Capacity -> enrollment -> revenue, never the reverse.** Do not enroll the seventh learner on the promise of coverage not yet secured.

## Cohort & rotation

- **One season live at a time.** The founding cohort moves together into The Forge. Do **not** run simultaneous seasons (not operable solo).
- **New 16-18s join The Forge in progress** - the spec's cycle/line model; they meet Origin later in the cycle, riding once fully around before they launch. The Forge is the *kindest* season to enter cold (outward at a shared problem, not baring an interior).
- **Cohort 4-7, target 5.** Grow toward 7 only if the one-deep-hour coverage is genuinely secured.
- **Mixed-age (15-18) is a gift here** - The Forge is collaborative and outward, so developmental diversity is a resource (the 18-year-old's systems-thinking scaffolds the 15-year-old; the 15-year-old's "why not?" un-jades the 18-year-old). Split the deep block by *readiness/appetite*, fluid, never age-labeled. Watch: don't let venture-scale work set a ceiling the younger learners measure against (the refusals hold harder in a mixed-age room).

### Build the welcome (do not ship it as a sentence) - the joiner scaffold

"Join in progress, never late" is aspiration until these are built (Jake + Ezri):
1. **A "Fast-Origin seed"** (~2-3 weeks, embedded in the joiner's self-guided project blocks - *not* a full parallel Origin): a rapid 100-Dreams / "what's yours," an agency inventory (power-of-choice), and a routine-that-holds. The three Origin functions The Forge silently stands on. Re-enterable, never a gate.
2. **The Captain's Log from day one** - "every Log starts empty; yours starts now, same first page." By lens, not page-count; no deficit read.
3. **A returner peer-witness pairing** (cross-cohort) - reciprocal witnessing, *not* tutoring: the joiner witnesses a returner's carried-forward dream; the returner witnesses the joiner naming theirs. Converts the tenure gap into a gift given-and-returned, and weaves the cohort.
4. **A shared threshold season-opener** the whole room crosses together (reset-for-all, not recap-for-her) + **no visible catch-up** (seed-work lives in the joiner's own blocks; in the shared deep hour she's a full Forge learner from day one).

### Make continuity real for the returner (Ezri)

The Log "deepens" only if the mechanism is built: **The Forge opens by reaching back into each returner's own Origin harvest** - "which of your 100 dreams is a problem you could act on? Which broke your heart enough to name?" Origin's harvest becomes The Forge's premise; the arc self->world made *personally* legible, not just structurally asserted. Offered, never obligated (a returner may carry a wholly new dream - sovereignty).

## The seven sessions (from the three-year spec; run at lighter-Forge intensity)

| # | Session | Shape | The work |
|---|---------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Naming the Dragon | short | Problem vs fact-of-life vs preference vs mystery; "Problem or Not?"; complaint -> "How Might We"; the Iceberg. *(Opens from the returners' Origin harvest.)* |
| 2 | The Detective | short | Root-cause / the 5 Whys; cohort picks a format; "A Death at Greywater Manor" -> "no one murdered her; a system did." |
| 3 | My Arena | short | Right-sizing (Covey's Circles); the Three Circles map; agency inventory; a starter quest - a win you can feel. |
| 4 | The Wider Field | short | Choose the problem - "what breaks your heart?" (Buechner); an expedition; the Problem Gallery. |
| 5 | The Long Game | long | Systems not single acts; proximal goals, leverage, collaboration, failing-forward; three doors (clowning / inner child / shadow-of-the-problem). |
| 6 | Hands to Work | long | Real action; a menu of how to serve (direct/advocacy/philanthropy/building/teaching), chosen; design *with*, not for. |
| 7 | The Call to Action | long, harvest | A public advocacy talk - Problem -> Stakes -> Story -> The Ask; vulnerability is the persuasive force; a feast. |

## Safety points distinctive to The Forge
- **S4 eco-anxiety/despair** - always pair the wider field with agency; hope, not helplessness; the expedition leaves them lit up, not crushed.
- **S2 the mystery** - keep it Clue-style, bloodless; opt-out; a lighter swap ready for any learner a murder lands wrong on.
- **S5 shadow work** - aimed at *the shadow of the problem, never forced excavation of the self*; opt-out; lightest touch, last.
- **S6 service** - do not place teens over their heads with vulnerable populations; service should stretch, not wound.
- **S7 the Call to Action** - advocacy, not a TED-performance; the guide holds testimony over applause.

## Forge v0.2 elevation (2026-08-24 fresh-eyes panel)

Five fresh voices converged on one finding: **The Forge positioned the learner *outside/above* the web - the mapper outside the map, the visitor not the member, the server not the served-alongside, the fixer vs the harmed, the technician above the living whole. The elevation weaves the learner *in*.** The same lesson the Closing Feast learned ("widening the table of us"), applied to the whole outward turn.

**Cross-cutting principle - the mapper is in the map:** wherever the learner meets a problem, add the standpoint question - *"Where are you inside this system? What does it let you see, and what does it hide? What do the people who live it see that you can't?"* Design-*with* becomes a truth understood, not a rule obeyed. (S3-S6.)

1. **The Commons Problem** (Ibn) - alongside each personal problem, the cohort deliberates ONE shared problem into being (S4) and carries it together to a *chorus* Call (S7). Civic formation by structure - a we, not five solos.
2. **The community-standing reciprocity gate** (Kasidy, mirrors the Closing Feast) - S6 service doesn't touch real people until a named community member is *invited and agrees*; the harvest sits with *their* verdict; benefit-flows-to-them named up front; a repair clause; affected voices lead early; no story in the S7 Call without consent.
3. **Love before heartbreak** (Mae) - S4 opens with *"what do you love about being alive on this planet?"* before the problems. Agency cures powerlessness; only love cures despair. + a living-system expedition site + grief honored (route to SSC).
4. **The 100-year relay** (Mae) - threaded S4-S7: *"one hand in a relay that began before you and continues after"* - "carry it my distance," not "I must solve it."
5. **S2's return trip** (Winona) - the arrow both ways: cause -> system AND system -> the choices and people who can answer (including you). Cause + accountability = the whole of justice.
6. **Systems rigor** (Vuna) - S2: 5 Whys *three times*, then find the *loop* (a system is a web, not a ladder). S5: a leverage-ladder (the obvious levers are weakest) + "if your action works, what pushes back?" (name an unintended consequence - the anti-savior antidote).
7. **Accountability-with-compassion + Call to Repair** (Winona) - S5 shadow: the people inside the shadow are inside the system too (repair, don't just defeat). S7: a *Call to Repair* ("here's what I owe, who leads, how you join them"), and the honest partial finding is the *high-status* Call, not a tolerated empty basket.

*Routing: service + grief pieces -> SSC (Salus/Bashir); community-relationship endings/continuity -> Ācārya Nhất.*
