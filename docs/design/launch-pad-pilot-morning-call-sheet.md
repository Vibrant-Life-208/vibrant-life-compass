**Why this exists:** the status document's own next step. *"The review body is closed. The next real step is not more review - it is one real Origin morning."*

## Call 1 - the clinician

**The one call that closes three separate owed items.** Ask for all three; they are the same conversation.

1. **Will you be the named warm-referral therapist?** Same-week handoff, a real name and a current number. This is the single blank in an otherwise complete crisis roster, it has been the blank for months, and **it is now the only thing standing between you and a pilot morning.** It is also a blank on a page that goes to families, so their answer has to be a yes you can print.
2. **The Table question** *(changed 2026-10-19: the studio does not provide the food - learners bring their own lunch. That changes the question rather than just the wording, so ask it the new way.)* A shared meal runs every guide day and everyone brings their own. Presence, never consumption, and nobody watches anybody's plate. For a learner in active eating-disorder recovery: **does bring-your-own help or hurt?** It hands them control of their own portion, which may be the better arrangement. It also makes what they chose visible on the table every time, which may be the worse one. You will know which. And what would you need us to do differently either way? *Ask also: is being at the Table refusable on a hard day without it reading as not belonging?*
3. **Who else should we point families to** when the honest answer is that this room cannot hold what their learner needs? Real names and numbers. Special education advocates, programmes with aide support, the state parent training centre. Six names is enough.

## Call 2 - the two adults who will watch you

**This call has no recipients yet, and that is the gate.** It previously named Salus and Jake. Those are fleet agents. They cannot stand in a room, cannot see a sixteen-year-old's face change, and cannot stop you mid-morning. Gate 7 was recorded closed against two personas, so the one finding nine circles converged on, an external adult with standing to tell you you are wrong, has never actually been staffed.

**Before this call can be made:** two human names. Each has to agree, out loud, to all of 1 through 5 below. Their names go on the Family note, which means a family can hold them to it, not only you.

1. **Confirm the date** once you have one, and confirm both are in the room for the whole morning.
2. **Get the standing permission explicitly.** Not *could you stop me*, which is a question. *You can stop this morning, without asking me first, and you do not have to explain it to me afterwards.* A watcher who needs the guide's permission to intervene is not a watcher.
3. **Name the reachable adult** for crisis concurrency that morning, on paper, in case something happens while you are with a learner. One name, one number.
4. **Say the watch out loud**, so they know what they are for. Not whether the morning goes well. Four things: does the guide *meet* the first no or *manage* it. Does the settle land in a real body. Could these learners freely decline. Could every learner have said yes in the first place.
5. **The honest frame, from Naomi:** this pilot cannot validate the sovereignty claim, because the first learners are the founder's child and the community's children, and they structurally cannot freely decline. So watch, do not conclude.

## Call 3 - the families

Two to four teenagers. No pitch and no persuasion.

- What the morning is, plainly, and that it is one morning rather than a commitment.
- That two other adults will be in the room and why.
- **That their learner may decline any part of it, including all of it, and nothing changes.**
- That nothing from the morning is photographed, written up or posted. Not now, not later.
- Consent is given against the one-page note, not against this phone call. Send or hand them the note after this call so they read it before they answer.

## Then: the date

Within about three weeks of the calls. Put it in the calendar before they go cold.

**The note goes out before the morning, and the morning does not wait on it.** Intervals below are Sisko's recommendation, not yet set.

- **Seven days before:** the filled-in note goes to each family, by hand or by email, with the date, your number, the clinician, the agency and both adults' names on it. Seven days rather than three. A page that says *it is not therapy and here is the honest ceiling* does its work over a dinner table, not in a doorway.
- **Forty-eight hours before:** the signed note is back. Not on the day.
- **If a note is not back forty-eight hours before, that learner does not come, and nobody is telephoned about it on the morning.** You will want to make an exception. Write the rule now, while no face is attached to it. Two to four learners means you can lose one to this and still have a morning, and that margin is what makes the rule real.

Consent given on the doorstep is not consent given against a note.

**One change to the morning itself, before a run-of-show exists.** `origin-lesson-plan-book.md` line 504 says Session 1 Week 1 is the whole week, and why: *"Agreements signed in the hour you met the room are compliance, not consent, so this week gives each rite its own air."* The design builds and walks the exit ramp **before** any promise is asked for. The pilot compresses that week into one morning, which means it is scheduled in the one hour the design says a no has nowhere to stand. **Put the exit ramp first inside the morning.** Build it and walk it once in practice, twenty minutes, before anything is asked. Then the Curious No is being tested against a no that has somewhere to land.
