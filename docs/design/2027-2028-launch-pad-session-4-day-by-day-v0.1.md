# Launch Pad Year 1 - Session 4 "Running My Own Ship" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft
**Dates:** Oct 18 - Nov 5, 2027 (weeks 10-12 of Origin)
**Companions:** Sessions 1-3 day-by-day; studio-year-plan v0.3 (Part 2 self-management; Part 4 credits); three-year spec

## The session at a glance

- **Arc:** OPEN (the reframe: routine makes freedom possible) → DEEPEN (build a routine that holds + communication practice) → APPLY (run it; the midpoint look-back + credit-pacing check) → HARVEST (a rhythm that fits, and a look at who you're becoming).
- **The thread underneath:** *freedom you can't hold is just noise. A routine that fits you is how you actually run your own ship.*
- **Lens:** Who am I? (now: the one at the helm). **Joy-flavor:** the quiet satisfaction of a rhythm that fits - a clear desk, real momentum.
- **The core teaching:** routine is **not the opposite of freedom - it is what makes freedom possible.** This is Session 3's choice, lived as a rhythm that fits *you* (not one imposed on you).
- **The five-region tie:** anchored in **Self** (energy, rhythm) and **Making** (getting real work done), with **Others** (communication) and **Voice** (the midpoint look-back: who am I becoming?).

## Why this session is a hinge (two things land here)

1. **Self-management gets formally taught and the wheels start coming off - per learner.** S4 is where the gradual-release scaffold (v0.3 Part 2) is deepened, and where you assess *each* learner's readiness to move from Phase 1 (structured) toward Phase 2 (guided freedom). Readiness is per-learner, never date-gated - a learner who still needs the full scaffold keeps it, without shame.
2. **The first formal credit-pacing checkpoint.** The Credit/Path Map (built in S1 wk2-3) gets its first real check against the plan - reframed, not pass/fail: *"what pace is real for you right now, and does the plan match it?"* and the re-askable goal: *"is finishing core this year still yours?"* (Part 4). Adjust the plan to the learner, never the learner to the plan.

### The care point (Deanna's, applied here)
The midpoint reflection is a **look at who you're becoming, not a shortfall audit.** Frame it toward growth and strengths - "what have these weeks shown you about yourself?" - not a ledger of what you didn't finish. A self-management session must build agency, not a private record of falling short. The credit check is a pacing conversation, witnessed, never graded.

## Notes and caveats

- **This is the natural readiness-gate moment** - assess each learner's self-management per-learner (v0.3 Part 2). Some move toward autonomy; some keep the full scaffold, without it reading as remedial.
- **The credit checkpoint is a pacing conversation, not a verdict** - reframed to "what pace is real," re-ask the goal, adjust the plan to the learner. A learner who needs the multi-year path names it now, not in April (Part 4).
- **The midpoint look-back is growth-framed** (Deanna) - who you're becoming, not what you didn't finish. Guard against it becoming a deficit ledger.
- **Election Day (Tue Nov 2)** - decide observance; flag for the real calendar (a guide day falls on it).
- **Communication practice** is the seed for the interviewing through-skill and Year 3's mentor outreach - keep it real (a genuine email, a genuine conversation), not role-play only.
- **The long haul is next** - S5-S7 are six-week sessions across the winter; a routine that holds is exactly what a learner needs to carry the Full Draft through the breaks. That's why routine lands here, right before the long voyage.
- **Still owed, not lifted here:** the consented Salus + Jake real-learner walk; the coach-not-therapist referral pathway in the guide spec.
