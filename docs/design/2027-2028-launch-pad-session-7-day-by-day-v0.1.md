## The session at a glance

- **Arc:** OPEN (what the Unveiling is, and the year it completes) → DEEPEN (make the "This Is Me" self-portrait) → APPLY (assemble the portfolio; prepare the exhibition) → HARVEST (the Unveiling feast).
- **The thread underneath:** *dreamed in Session 1, drafted in Session 5, shaped in Session 6 - now declared. This is me.*
- **Lens:** Who am I? - *lived with, and shown* - a truer, bigger question than when the year began (not closed). **Joy-flavor (the guide's private hope, never the aim):** some feel deeply seen; some feel exposed or flat - both are honored, and no one performs the feeling.
- **The bookend (make the year rhyme):** the 100 Dreams (S1) → the Full Draft (S5) → Chapter One (S6) → the Unveiling (S7). If the closing anthem is "This Is Me," the year rhymes end to end.
- **The form:** a self-portrait / art piece - "This Is Me" - in whatever medium is true to the learner (art, film, music, writing, spoken word, mixed). Presented with the story-of-self portfolio, the resume page, and Chapter One.
- **The five-region tie:** the self-portrait declares the whole self - all five regions at once, with **Voice** (the authoring self) at the center.

## The care point (being seen is invitational)

The Unveiling is a celebration, and being seen can be exposing - especially for the anxious or isolated learner. Hold this:
- **The learner chooses how much they declare** - the piece and the telling are theirs; depth is invitational, never a performance requirement.
- **Insider sovereignty** - a learner whose story is tender sets the terms of what travels to the room.
- **The audience is warm - your people, not a panel.** A feast, not a judged presentation; the cohort cheers each other.
- **A real alternative shape, not just "share less"** *(Deanna)*: unveil to the tribe only; or in a written/recorded form the guide presents; or to one chosen person - each equal, not a lesser path. Invitation to a single fixed event is thinner than invitation to *how* you are seen.
- **The family-less learner is named and held** *(Polaris)*: the Unveiling is a real-audience family event, and the learner whose family will not or cannot come is the one most exposed by "show your people." **The tribe is your people**; a chosen witness (a mentor, a tribe-mate's family who adopts you for the night) is honored *equally*, never as consolation. Do not let "families come" become a floor a family-less learner falls through on the year's most tender day.
- **Hold the year's close gently** - this ends the first year of a three-year container. Name it, let it be a little tender; the story continues (next year: The Forge). *(The full "Launch" exit is Year 3; here it's a year-close, not a goodbye.)*
- Coach-not-therapist still applies (the piece can surface a lot).
