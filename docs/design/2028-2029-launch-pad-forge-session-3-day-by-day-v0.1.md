# Launch Pad Year 2 (The Forge) - Session 3 "My Arena" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft | **When:** weeks 7-9 (short session, 3 weeks)
**Companions:** Year 2 studio-year plan (Forge); Forge Sessions 1-2; three-year spec (Season Two)

## The session at a glance
- **Arc:** OPEN (right-sizing) -> DEEPEN (the Three Circles + agency inventory) -> APPLY (a starter quest - one small real problem, acted on) -> HARVEST (a win you can feel: "this one is mine, and I can move it").
- **The thread:** *energy spent only in the Circle of Concern burns out into cynicism; the shift is to find the arena where your action actually moves something.*
- **Ties back:** to Year 1's Power of Choice - the move from "problems happen to me" to "I can act on this slice." (Returners have it; joiners built the agency-inventory in their Fast-Origin seed - a clean convergence.)
- **Lighter-Forge week:** Tue guided-lighter (the mapping/inventory + starter-quest planning), Thu guided-deep (the Socratic right-sizing). Mon/Wed self-guided; Fri fallow.

## Care points
- **The anti-despair move is the whole session** - a learner drowning in world-sized problems is handed a *slice they can move.* Right-sizing protects against both cynicism (only-Concern) and burnout (trying to hold it all).
- **Deficit-guard:** a small arena is not a small person. Acting on one real thing in your reach is the win, at any scale. "The quiet arena is the invitation."

## Week 7 - OPEN (the Three Circles)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; list everything about your dragon you worry about; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; **the Three Circles map** (Concern / Influence / Control) - sort your worries; watch where energy can actually act. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; sit with your map; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - right-sizing, Socratically: where does your energy move something, and where does it just burn? Concern-only -> cynicism. Bridge: what do you actually have to act with?
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 8 - DEEPEN (agency inventory + starter quest)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; agency inventory (skills, time, relationships, resources you have *now*); Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; finish the agency inventory; **pick a starter quest** - one small real problem inside your arena to act on. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; scope your starter quest small enough to actually finish; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - the move from "it happens to me" to "I can act on this slice"; test the starter quest against the Three Circles (is it really in your Influence/Control?). Bridge: begin acting.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 9 - APPLY -> HARVEST (a win you can feel)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - act on the starter quest; core; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; act / build / share starter-quest progress in company. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - finish the starter quest; core; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): HARVEST** - a **win you can feel** - "this one is mine, and I moved it" (the self-efficacy spiral). Voluntary share. *Empty-basket honored: a starter quest that didn't work is data about the arena, never a verdict on you.* Bridge into Session 4 (The Wider Field): *"You've moved something in your arena. Now lift your eyes - what in the wider world breaks your heart?"*
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 3.**

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
