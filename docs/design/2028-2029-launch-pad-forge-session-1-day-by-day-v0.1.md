# Launch Pad Year 2 (The Forge) - Session 1 "Naming the Dragon" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft (+ Ring-1 seeds 2026-08-28)
**When:** the opening 3 weeks of The Forge (weeks 1-3 of 2028-2029; exact dates mapped to the school calendar once set)
**Companions:** Year 2 studio-year plan (Forge) v0.1; the three-year spec (Season Two); Year 1 session docs (the format)

## The session at a glance

- **Arc:** OPEN (the year's threshold + the distinction) -> DEEPEN (name it precisely) -> APPLY (reframe your own) -> HARVEST (name the dragon you'll carry).
- **The thread underneath:** *a vague dragon ("everything sucks") cannot be fought; a named one can. Naming precisely is half the victory.*
- **Ring-1 seeds (2026-08-28):** the through-lines carry from Origin - the Closing Practice (now a year old; `launch-pad-closing-practice-v0.1.md`), the Vineyard, the graduated somatic floor; decline-with-reasoning opens the season as a covenant. Name the destination on day one: "in ~30 weeks, the Call to Action." And **this is the year the Studio Commons launches** (its smallest-true-thing - pooled Fund + pay-stub/W-4 machinery; see the strand spec) - the founding cohort now has a year of trust and the child is in Tots.
- **The distinction this session teaches** (the thing nobody hands teenagers): a **problem** (you can act on it) vs a **fact of life** (accept it) vs a **preference** (you just want it) vs a **mystery** (sit with it). You fight each differently.
- **Signature ritual begins:** the **Impossible Question** opens each guided day (a question with no clean answer, debated playfully) - replacing Year 1's choice games.
- **This session also opens the year** - so it carries the shared threshold, the returner reach-back, and the joiner scaffold (below).

## Opening the year (built into Week 1)

- **A shared threshold - reset-for-all, not recap-for-the-new-kid.** The whole room crosses the same doorway: who we are, what The Forge asks ("what is worth solving?"), the year ahead. Returners re-enter (they've had a summer; they're not the same people); joiners enter for the first time - in the *same* opener, never a remedial side-door.
- **The returner reach-back:** returners re-open their Origin Captain's Log and **pull one dream forward** - "which of your 100 dreams is a problem you could act on? which broke your heart enough to name?" Origin's harvest becomes The Forge's premise. Offered, never obligated (a returner may carry a wholly new dream).
- **The joiner scaffold** (so "join in progress" is real, not a euphemism for behind):
  - **The Captain's Log from day one** - "every Log starts empty; yours starts now, same first page."
  - **A returner peer-witness pairing** (reciprocal, not tutoring): the joiner witnesses a returner's carried-forward dream; the returner witnesses the joiner naming theirs.
  - **A "Fast-Origin seed"** in the joiner's self-guided blocks across weeks 1-3 (~2-3 weeks, embedded, re-enterable, *not* a full parallel Origin): a rapid 100-Dreams / "what's yours," an agency inventory, a routine-that-holds - the three Origin functions The Forge stands on.
  - **No visible catch-up** - seed-work lives in the joiner's own blocks; in the shared deep hour, a full Forge learner from day one.

## The care points for this session
- **The distinction itself is the care.** Naming something a *fact of life* (accept it) vs a *problem* (act on it) protects a learner from burning agency on the unchangeable - and from the despair of treating everything as their fault to fix. Hold that gently, especially for a learner whose hard things are genuinely facts-of-life, not problems they failed to solve.
- **Guard the deficit read** (carries from Year 1): a learner whose whole world feels like "facts of life" they can't change is not deficient - naming even one small problem in their arena is the win. "The quiet arena is the invitation."
- Coach-not-therapist: complaints can surface real pain; hold, don't treat; route if it's past coaching.

## Week 1 - OPEN (the threshold + the distinction)

- **Mon - Self-guided** - a settle-in first day. Returners re-open their Origin Log; joiners begin the Fast-Origin seed; all: core academics rhythm restarts. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter): THE THRESHOLD** - the shared reset-for-all opener; the first **Impossible Question** ("Can one person change a system?"); peer-witness pairings assigned; the year framed. Close with the returner reach-back invitation (bring a dream forward Thursday). *Toddler-flexible day.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - returners: pull a dream forward from the Origin harvest; joiners: Fast-Origin seed continues; all: notice one thing that bugs you this week. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): the distinction** - the deep block. Problem / fact-of-life / preference / mystery. **"Problem or Not?"** sorting - real situations sorted into the four buckets; *the arguments over the gray cards are the lesson.* Begin the Iceberg (surface vs the thing underneath). Bridge: watch for your own dragon.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 2 - DEEPEN (name it precisely)

- **Mon - Self-guided** - reframe one of your own complaints; core; Log; joiner seed continues. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; **Reframe-It -> "How Might We"** as a group game (crank a complaint - "history is pointless" - into an open design question - "how might we make this matter?"). A complaint is a closed door; a How-Might-We is an open one. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - apply the Iceberg to a real complaint (what's underneath it?); core; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): where's the dragon really?** - the deep block. Naming precisely: a vague dragon can't be fought, a named one can. Each learner brings a candidate problem and works to name it *precisely* - not "everything sucks" but the specific, actionable thing. Bridge: refine your named dragon.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 3 - APPLY -> HARVEST (name the dragon you'll carry)

- **Mon - Self-guided** - refine your candidate problem into a clean How-Might-We; core; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; share candidate dragons; find "problem-neighbors" (whose problem rhymes with yours - the seed of collaboration); light peer feedback. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - finalize your How-Might-We; core; Log; come ready Thursday. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): HARVEST** - each learner names **the dragon they'll carry** - a precisely-named problem / How-Might-We to trace and act on across the season. A small voluntary share. *Empty-basket honored: "I'm still searching for mine" is a true and honored harvest - a named problem can't be forced.* Bridge into Session 2 (The Detective): *"You've named the dragon. Next: trace it to its root - the obvious villain is almost never the cause."*
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 1.**

## Notes and caveats
- **The joiner's Fast-Origin seed spans this whole session** (weeks 1-3) in their self-guided blocks - re-enterable, never a gate; by the harvest, a joiner has enough "what's mine" to name a dragon that's genuinely theirs.
- **The returner reach-back is the continuity mechanism** - the Origin harvest becomes the Forge premise; don't open the year with a fresh framework and a clean slate, or the Captain's Log becomes a scrapbook.
- **The deep block is once a week (Thu)** - the lighter-Forge shape; the Tue guided day carries the games/sorting/social work that's toddler-flexible.
- **Carries from Year 1:** the six refusals, the Log privacy, the Table, coach-not-therapist, per-learner readiness, the accessibility doors, the fallow Friday.
- **Dates** map to the 2028-2029 calendar once set (like Year 1).
