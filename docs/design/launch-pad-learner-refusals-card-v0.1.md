## Six promises, and one on top

**These are promises to you. You can hold us to them.**

Nothing in the Launch Pad is allowed to break the six below. They are not rules you follow. They are things we owe you, and this page is yours to keep so that you never have to take anyone's word for what they were.

---

**1. No grades or scores anyone but you can see.** Your progress is yours.

**2. No ranking and no leaderboards.** You are never stacked against the person next to you. If that ever stops being exactly true, you will be told before it changes, not after.

**3. No one deposits the answers into you.** You make the meaning here. The guide holds questions and walks with you, and does not hand you a finished you.

**4. No imposed deadlines.** You set your own pace. "I didn't get to it" is honored, not punished.

**5. Your worth is not your output.** A quest you abandoned and learned from counts as much as one you finished. Doing more does not make you worth more.

**6. No surveillance.** The data of your life belongs to you, not to the school.

---

### And one that sits on top of the six

**You may refuse the structure itself.** Decline a whole quest and you are fully honored, not quietly marked down as a failure or an opt-out.

**Your no is real.** It lands the moment you say it, it costs you nothing, and nobody can overrule it.

---

### Where your no reaches, and where it stops

**It covers your own work.** A quest, a prompt, an interview, a share, a whole session, this entire structure.

**It does not cover how you treat the people in the room with you.** That is the floor that keeps this room safe for six other people, and it is the one place here where something can actually be taken from you. The studio's agreements run that floor, and you help write them in your first week. The whole ladder is written down where you can read it.

In one line: **the agreements are about how we treat each other. The promises are about whether you do the work.**

---

*Declining a quest is never a Pause. A Pause is never a judgement on your work, your pace, or whether you are keeping up. If anyone ever runs those two together, they have got it wrong, and you can say so out loud and point at this page.*
