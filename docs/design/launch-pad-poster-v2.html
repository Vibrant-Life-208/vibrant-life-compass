Not a high school.

The Launch Pad is a three-year studio at Vibrant Life for learners aged 16 to 18. There are no grades, no rankings, and no deadlines the learner didn't set. Nobody here is sorted.

Two mornings a week the learners work with a guide. Two mornings they run themselves. Friday is never filled, on purpose, and that is in the plan rather than a thing we let slip.

The first year asks one question, Who am I? The second asks What is worth solving? The third asks What do I do when there is no easy answer? Each learner keeps a Log through all three years. It is theirs. No one reads it but them.

Here is what one guide can hold, and here is what she can't: it is one guide, there is no aide, and on guide mornings her baby is in the room. The room holds a handful of learners. We would rather you hear that from us than find it out.

The whole three years is printed, every page written so a sixteen-year-old can read it. Ask for a copy. Then come sit in the room one evening, try a morning yourselves, and decide. If you'd rather not, nothing changes between us.
