## Cross-cutting findings (all four courses)

These held true across every course and are the strongest part of the current design:

1. **Read-only, no persistence, no count.** The course surface (`whereToStartSection`) renders the
   arc + a start card + the path as guidance; only the WOOP is a persisted object. *Jake:* "no count
   anywhere, ever" is satisfied structurally, not just as a remembered rule — that is a wall, not a
   promise. *Salus:* no product metric on the course surface (my Clabough non-negotiable) — confirm no
   "steps viewed" / completion counter is ever added; the moment one is, the surface drifts from
   guidance into analytics.
2. **Invitational entry, no placement quiz.** "Not sure where to begin?" with one first move and the
   arc behind it — no "you're behind." *Salus:* the safe default is the right default. *Jake:*
   exploration before commitment — good.
3. **Capacity, never a score or a title or a number.** All four name a capacity, not an outcome.
   *Jake:* authenticity over achievement — held. *Both:* this is the spine that keeps the courses out
   of the performance-orientation frame.
4. **The 12-18 register is honest about its own limit.** The 8-11 young rewrite is deferred to Wave-2,
   not shipped silently. *Jake:* developmental diversity is real — an 8-year-old is concrete-
   operational; the abstract steps (margin of safety, price-it-fair, self-compassion framing) are
   correctly held for the older register.

**Two conditions both reviewers attach to ALL four before any flag-lift:**
- **(C-ALL-1) The real-learner walk.** Binding, un-substitutable. Salus watches for refusability and
  wince; Jake watches for developmental landing and evaluative leak. Run them jointly, as one
  supervised walk (their standing proposal).
- **(C-ALL-2) Loud skip / "not now" with no penalty.** *Jake:* "skip must be the LOUD default for the
  young or it is compliance, not choice." *Salus:* opt-in everything; never require a child to opt
  into protection. No step may gate another. The read-only design already supports this; confirm the
  young rewrite keeps it.

## Financial Literacy (Keep → Earn → Grow & Guard)

**Salus: SAFE, one CAUTION.** The course avoids the extraction script cleanly (capacity-not-a-number,
no "millionaire," beneficence keystone, non-judgmental need/want sort). *The one CAUTION:* "set aside
a tenth" / "name your free-with" can land on a child in a scarce household as a reminder of what they
do not have — the Hill cruelty of moralizing the state of having little, which the money report itself
names as the line never to cross. "However small" softens it; it is not yet enough.
- **Condition (F-1):** the framing must never assume the child *has* money. Add an honest path for the
  child with nothing to set aside (the act of *deciding* the tenth, before there is anything to apply
  it to) so the step is doable by the poorest learner without shame.

**Jake: SUPPORTIVE.** "The tenth" is concrete and doable (right for the register). Overjustification
caveat honored (never pay a child for a passion they love). Circle-of-competence / margin-of-safety
are abstract — correct to hold for Wave-2 at the younger tier.
- **Condition (F-2):** the young rewrite must not carry the abstract Stage-3 mechanics down unglanced.

**Joint disposition: CLEAR-WITH-CONDITIONS** (F-1, F-2, + C-ALL-1/2).

## Leadership (Serve → Steady → Gather)

**Salus: SAFE.** Lowest trauma-adjacency of the four. "Serve without the credit — notice whether you
can" and "Own one miss" are gentle self-observation, not exposure. No condition beyond C-ALL.

**Jake: SUPPORTIVE, near OPTIMAL.** This is his register. Servant-first defuses the identity-
foreclosure risk he guards against (a *direction*, not a title — exploration before commitment).
"Make more servant-leaders, not followers" and "hand it on" are the anti-dependency / removability
tell (Kohn: a real scaffold is built to become unnecessary). Prosociality-is-protective is embodied,
not asserted. The Greene/Machiavelli specimen firewall he endorses.
- **Note (L-1), not a condition:** "Name who you'd serve — what you'd carry even when it costs you" is
  identity-weighted; invitational framing is correct, watch at the walk that it doesn't land as a test
  for a younger teen.

**Joint disposition: CLEAR-WITH-CONDITIONS** (lightest of the four; C-ALL-1/2 + watch L-1 at the walk).

## Wellness (Tend → Be Kind → Connect) — HIGHEST RISK

**Salus: CAUTION → CONCERN pending the human trauma pass.** This is exactly her eating-disorder-audit
territory (her 2026-03-03 audit: "CONTRAINDICATED means remove, not reword"). The draft has the
AED/AAP guardrail genuinely baked in — no weight, no counting, no good/bad food, body-appreciation
(what your body lets you *do*), and one step that *drops* a number. That is unusually careful; it may
be the best-guarded wellness copy she has read. **And that is precisely when she trusts her own
certification least.** Specific reads:
- Step 1.4 "Eat something that nourishes you," and 1.1 "Sleep one night well": even framed as care,
  *any* food or body directive can be an optimization/control vector for an ED-vulnerable youth. Her
  standing rule is that these are not spec-clearable — they need a clinician's eyes on a real
  child-facing surface, not a reword.
- Step 2.3 (the firewall — name the wellness-industry sell): she endorses strongly; it is
  evoke-not-extract embodied and protective.
- Step 2.4 "Drop one number": directionally right, but for a kid already tracking, being *told* to drop
  it can spotlight it — watch at the walk.
- Confirmed absent (good): no long body-scan (her 7/22 "MYRIAD harm signal for at-risk youth"
  exclusion), no cold-water, no tapping.

**Jake: CONCERNING → SUPPORTIVE with condition.** Self-compassion-not-self-esteem is well-grounded
adolescent developmental science; connection-central is his register (relationships as the
developmental engine). No count — "quality, not count" is his "notice, never score" verbatim.
- **Condition (W-1):** the Connect steps ("Reach one person today," "Belong to something") can land as
  a measure-of-failure on a socially-anxious or isolated teen who *can't* today. They need the loud
  "not now, and that's not a failure" default beside them, not one tap away (his standing shape; Salus's
  "protection sits where the pressure lands, not in the fine print").

**Joint disposition: HOLD-FOR-HUMAN.** Wellness does not clear on a spec read. The ED axis is
Salus's non-negotiable clinician surface; W-1 is a real fix. This is the one to read hardest, and the
one whose flag must not lift until the human trauma-informed pass runs and the joint walk passes.

## Entrepreneurship (See → Make → Serve)

**Salus: CAUTION.** Lower trauma-adjacency than Wellness, not zero. "Put it in front of one real
person and watch what happens" is an exposure-to-rejection moment; the iterate framing ("improve from
what happened") cushions it but does not yet name the safety net out loud. And the kidfluencer /
audience-capture axis is a genuine safety concern for a minor (parasocial + self-as-product exposure).
- **Condition (E-1):** make failure-as-data explicit — a thing people don't want is information, never
  a verdict on the child. Salus wants the shock-absorber beside the exposure step, not implied.
- **Condition (E-2):** an explicit "you are not the product" beat — the course must never, at any age,
  drift toward "build an audience / monetize yourself." (The current firewall step — refuse the
  stickiness shortcut — already inoculates against this; E-2 makes it load-bearing for the young
  rewrite, where the kidfluencer pull is strongest.)

**Jake: SUPPORTIVE.** Rich, mostly excellent developmental territory. "Make the smallest real thing →
ship → improve from what happened" *is* his standing position #4 (failure is data) operationalized;
build→ship→learn is authentic-becoming through doing. "Refuse the hustle story / survivorship bias" he
endorses (anti-achievement-orientation, Kohn-adjacent). His sharpest concern is the same as Salus's:
the kidfluencer trap. His parasocial-measurement governance (no child metrics; children's parasocial
measures unready for the field) makes self-as-product a HARMFUL vector for minors *if it ever creeps
in* — the current draft does not push it (it is need-serving, not attention-capturing), and E-2 seals
it. "Trade fair / price it" is abstract-operational — correct to hold for Wave-2 at the younger tier.

**Joint disposition: FIX-LIST** (E-1, E-2, + C-ALL-1/2). Not a hold; two real copy fixes then walk.
