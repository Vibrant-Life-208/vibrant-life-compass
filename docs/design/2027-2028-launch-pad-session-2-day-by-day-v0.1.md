**Status:** v0.1 working draft

## The session at a glance

- **Arc:** OPEN (what is lineage) → DEEPEN (learn to gather a story) → APPLY (write it, prepare to share) → HARVEST (the family-story potluck).
- **The thread underneath:** *you come from somewhere and someone, and an ordinary life carries enormous meaning.*
- **Lens:** Who am I? (continued - now: whose story am I part of?). **Joy-flavor:** roots & belonging.
- **The five-region tie:** lineage dreams cluster in **Others** (family, the people who made you) and **Voice** (Legacy, roots, the story you carry). A good place to fill regions that came up thin in Session 1.
- **This session's Closing Feast is the family-story potluck itself** - the "where you come from" table. Per the Closing Feast plan, S2 is your *own* heritage; the *later* sessions go outward to neighbors' tables. The year widens the circle of "us" starting here, from home.

### The binding safety net (from the three-year spec) - hold this all session
- **Family = who made you.** Biological, chosen, or found - a grandparent, a mentor, a coach, a sibling, a neighbor all count. Never assume a two-parent, known-lineage default.
- **Opt-out on any interview, any memory, with no explanation owed.** Some lives carry loss; some carry rupture.
- **The mortality episode is previewed, with a real place to step out *to*** (see Day 1).
- **The potluck is belonging, not performance** - a store-bought dish is fully welcome.

### Empty-state dignity (Stirps's condition) - built in, not bolted on
"Where you come from" must have a graceful path for the learner whose heritage is **interrupted, mixed, adopted, or unknown**. Offer these as equal, celebrated options from Day 1, never as a fallback:
- "A recipe you wish you had - recreated from memory."
- "The food of the place you live now."
- "The meal your family makes so often no one needs the recipe."
- *Interrupted lineage is still lineage. However you arrive, you belong at this table.*

### Insider-learner sovereignty
A learner whose heritage is rich, painful, or on display chooses their own standing - share fully, share a little, or simply be present. Never turned into the exhibit. And **name specific peoples** (Eritrean, Nepali, Basque, Diné), never a regional lump.

## Notes and caveats

- **The mortality episode is the sharpest safety point** - preview, name the content, real exit, opt-out with no explanation. Some learners are in fresh grief; watch the room (this is the guide-as-life-coach's held presence, and the coach-not-therapist boundary applies - route anything clinical to real help).
- **The family interview can surface rupture** - estrangement, loss, adoption questions, unknown parents. The empty-state paths and the "family = who made you" frame exist for exactly this; keep them visible, not buried.
- **Insider sovereignty at the potluck** - a learner from a marginalized or painful lineage is never the exhibit; they set the terms.
- **Naming discipline** - specific peoples, always; each family names itself.
- **The potluck's inclusion floor** ties to the Closing Feast v0.2 (base provided, no cover charge, allergen/ED-safe) - it applies even to the "bring a dish" potluck: no learner is exposed for what they can or cannot bring. **The material-poverty path** *(Polaris, evoked-polish)*: the studio-provided base dish can quietly *be* a learner's contribution - pre-arranged so a learner who brings nothing is indistinguishable from one who brought a dish. Presence, not provision.
- **Still owed, not lifted here:** the consented Salus + Jake real-learner walk; the coach-not-therapist referral pathway in the guide spec.

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
