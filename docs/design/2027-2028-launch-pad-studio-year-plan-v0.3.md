## Part 2 - The guide-led day, designed around the baby (Tue/Thu, 9:00-1:00)

The design principle, since there is **no relief adult on guide days**: the day has exactly **one** block that needs Erin's whole, uninterruptible presence (the deep Socratic work). Everything else is baby-flexible. So the one demanding block is protected three ways - fed before, timed to the nap, and given a built-in pivot.

| Time | Block | Baby-fit |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00-9:20 | Walk-in & soft start - arrive at own pace, self-directed settle | Low-verbal window; a natural time to nurse/settle before the day's demands |
| 9:20-9:45 | Opening Circle - body-first settle + a *varied* "how are you, really?" | Baby usually worn and calm |
| 9:45-10:00 | The Quest Frame - open the quest as a question | **Feed here**, just before the deep block, so she's full and likely to nap through it |
| 10:00-11:00 | **The Work of the Day - the deep Socratic block** | Timed to ride the nap. **Built-in pivot (no outside relief):** if she can't settle, pause, tend her, and shift the room to a group challenge / self-work / the season's writing. The deep conversation resumes next guide day - postpone depth, never fake it. |
| 11:00-11:30 | The Table - shared meal, baby worn at the center | A soft block; nurse here if needed. A sleeping baby at a family table is belonging amplified, not compromised |
| 11:30-12:30 | Apply & Collaborate - hands-on / group work | Lower guide-intensity; baby-flexible |
| 12:30-1:00 | The Bridge + a *varied* Glad Close - build the next self-guided day's plan | Baby-flexible. Break for lunch/outside play at 1:00 |

**Why this holds without a relief body:** the room only needs Erin's *whole* attention for one hour, and that hour is placed where a fed, worn 3-month-old is most likely asleep. On the days that fails, the pivot means the cost is "the deep conversation waits two days," not "the day collapses." A small cohort makes the room easy to flex in the moment; older learners in a mixed-age room can hold a five-minute pause while she settles the baby - that is room-maturity, not childcare.

**At 6-7 learners, split the deep block.** A genuine Socratic conversation across seven teenagers spanning 15-18 is too wide for one guide, and it flexes badly on a hard baby day. So at 6+, run the deep block as two smaller circles: one does the Socratic work with you (3-4 learners) while the other does self-directed writing or a group challenge, then swap. Smaller circles hold depth better *and* let you step aside for the baby without the whole room stalling.

**Going deep - the three modes** (Socratic questioning; real conversation; group challenges) and the discussion culture (pass always allowed; disagree with the idea not the person; "I changed my mind" is a win; draw out the quiet voice) carry forward from v0.2, plus the **access doors in**: write-first-then-share think-time, a non-verbal contribution channel each day, "place yourself" never "stand," and a varied settle question.

**The Table inclusion floor** (from v0.2, unchanged): base meal always provided, no cover charge; allergen/cultural/religious safety planned; presence not consumption (no one watches a plate - a specific reason the Salus + Jake walk matters); Erin eats seated, same food; ritual bones (a threshold, a rotating role, screen-free as a gift).

Connection is not universally nourishing; for some, solitude is the safe base from which connection later becomes possible.

## Part 4 - High school core credits (for the learner who chooses it)

Unchanged from v0.2, in brief: **for a learner who names core-credit completion as high on their journey, Year 1 is the year to finish** (conditional, not a blanket "must"). The goal is **re-askable** at every monthly checkpoint ("is this still yours?"). A **different-path learner** gets a designed Block 1 (their chosen path-work), not a blank. A **dignified multi-year path** exists for a learner whose pace is a function of a learning difference. Credit audit in S1 weeks 2-3 (not Day 2 morning). Monthly checkpoint reframed to "what pace is real, and does the plan match it?" - witnessed, never graded.

**What Block 1 (core academics) actually is** *(evoked-polish - Naomi & Cura's #1: the unwatched door where school-as-usual survives)*: the ordinary academic block runs under the **same six refusals as everything else.** It is **self-paced mastery toward credits the learner has claimed** - not assigned, graded, or ranked seatwork on the guide's clock. The learner owns the pace; the guide witnesses and helps, never deposits or evaluates. The actual academic *curriculum/content* is a **separate deliverable** (and the one a parent will test first - name it honestly as not-yet-built). But on the page, Block 1 lives under the refusals, so the ordinary half of the week is as evoked as the luminous half.

## Part 6 - Sustainability and contingency (rewritten for the real model)

The daily Morning Steward is gone. What remains is smaller and specific.

### The one real pinch, and its plan

The deep Socratic hour is the only block that can't absorb a baby meltdown. It is protected by: (1) a feed right before it, (2) timing to the nap, (3) the built-in pivot (shift to group work, resume the depth next guide day). **This is the contingency, since there is no relief adult on Tue/Thu.** It works because depth is a rhythm across ~12 guide days per long session, not a daily do-or-die.

### The December contingency (name it now)

The **3-4 month sleep regression** will likely land around December - inside S5 (the Full Draft), stacking with the winter-break re-threading. Plan the soft landing now, while rested: expect more pivots that month, keep S5's guide days lighter-scope, and lean on the "convert to self-guided, postpone depth" fallback more freely. This is a normal season with a four-month-old, not a failure.

### Nursing in the grid

A 3-month-old nurses every 2-3 hours. On guide days (9:00-1:00) that is at least one feed inside the window - placed at 9:45 (before the deep block) and again at The Table (11:00) if needed. Both are soft blocks. Never across the Socratic hour.

### Whole-day absences (the baby's sick day, Erin's sick day)

Babywearing covers the normal day. It does **not** cover the day the baby is too sick to come, or Erin is. For those, keep the lighter version of the bench:
- **A short list of two adults** who can hold a self-guided day's presence (not teach) if Erin must be out entirely.
- **The substitute protocol:** a guide-led day with Erin out **converts to a self-guided day** (learners already know how); depth postponed, not faked.
- These are for *whole absences*, a handful of times a year - not daily coverage.

### Locked Decision #6 with teeth - see *The Guide's Own Floor* (companion doc)

- **Erin's Friday is fallow too** - protected, not on-call.
- **A witness-debrief, more often than monthly** - the S5/S7 witness-load (holding teens' worst days and self-portraits) needs a real reflective/peer debrief, not an occasional call. Coach-not-therapist applies *to the guide too*: she needs somewhere to set down what she witnesses.
- **A pre-committed pause-trigger, N=2:** if the deep work has been pivoted-away for **2 consecutive guide days** (baby or exhaustion), pause to fallow-mode and re-plan rather than push through. Pre-committed *now, while rested* - a depleted person does not pause; that is exactly when the instinct fails.
- **Permission, in writing:** a pivoted-away deep block or a good-enough December day is *the design working, not the guide failing.* Origin, not Mastery - the permission the learners get, the guide takes too.
- Full detail: **`2027-2028-launch-pad-the-guides-own-floor-v0.1.md`.**

## Evoked-polish principles (2026-08-23 review - cross-cutting, apply to all sessions)

From the 5-voice "are we serving / make it evoked" review. These apply across every session; the sharpest session-specific instances are edited in the session docs.

- **Feelings are the guide's private hope, never the session's aim** *(Guinan, Polaris, Deanna)*. The "joy-flavor" lines ("the relief and catharsis," "being seen") name what *some* learners may feel - never a deliverable. You cannot schedule "being seen" for a Thursday. Word every joy-flavor as possibility ("some feel this; some feel nothing yet; both are true"), never as the outcome.
- **The empty basket is a true harvest** *(Guinan, Naomi)*. Every harvest day carries an explicit honored outcome: *"I have nothing to declare this season" is a real and honored harvest - the fallow field is part of the crop.* No one performs a harvest on schedule.
- **Guard the self-as-deficit read** *(Deanna, Naomi)*. Learner-facing framing never renders a life as a gap-map: "the quiet regions are the invitation," not "the gap is the data." The Credit/Path Map is the learner's own, co-computed, non-cumulative across years. Season goals are droppable with no failure.
- **Leave one cup empty** *(Guinan)*. Build at least one genuinely unstructured, un-harvested stretch *inside* the arc (not just Fridays) - a fallow field with no Work of the Day, where the self assembles in the gap. Candidate: a designated open week in the long S5, or a lighter December week named as fallow-on-purpose.
- **A window, not only mirrors** *(Deanna, Guinan)*. The S5-S7 interior stretch (18 weeks of self-work) gets an outward-facing beat before the Unveiling - *witness someone else's story* (interview an elder, reflect a peer's Chapter One) - so being-seen becomes reciprocal, not performative. Built into S7's open.
- **A door for the learner who is not a story** *(Guinan)*. The year assumes the self is a narrative. Offer a dignified path for the learner who does not experience themselves that way - the self-portrait may be non-narrative (an image, a practice, a set of values) with equal honor.
- **Hand the learner the design** *(Polaris, Naomi)*. Name the "engines" to the learner where naming honors them (the guide holds your dreams *with* you, not for you); end the year with a co-authoring close and a **Log ownership handoff** ("it's yours, take it, no copy kept").
