# Launch Pad Year 2 (The Forge) - Session 7 "The Call to Action" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft | **When:** weeks 25-30 (long session, 6 weeks; the year's harvest)
**Companions:** Year 2 studio-year plan (Forge); Forge Sessions 1-6; three-year spec (Season Two)

## The session at a glance
- **Arc:** OPEN (what a Call to Action is) -> DEEPEN (Problem -> Stakes -> Story -> The Ask) -> APPLY (build + rehearse the talk) -> HARVEST (the public talk + a feast).
- **The thread:** *here is a problem worth solving, here is why it broke my heart, here is what I did and learned, here is what I ask of you. Advocacy, not a sales pitch - the art is making a room care.*
- **The structure to teach (simply):** Problem -> Stakes -> Story -> The Ask. **Vulnerability is the persuasive force, not polish.**
- **Lighter-Forge week:** Tue guided-lighter (talk-building, structure games, rehearsal in the tribe - toddler-flexible), Thu guided-deep (the Socratic shaping - what makes a room actually care). Mon/Wed self-guided; Fri fallow.

## Care points (carry the Year-1 S7 evoked-polish)
- **Being seen is invitational.** The talk and how much you share are the learner's; depth is never a performance requirement.
- **A real alternative shape, not just "share less":** the Call to Action can be given to the tribe only, or as a written/recorded form the guide presents, or to one chosen person - each equal, not a lesser path.
- **The family-less learner is named and held:** the audience is a warm one - the tribe is your people; a chosen witness is honored equally, never a consolation.
- **Advocacy, not a TED-performance:** the guide holds *testimony* over applause; vulnerability is the force, not polish. Never coach for the room's reaction over the learner's conviction.
- Coach-not-therapist carries; empty-basket honored.

## Week 25 - OPEN (what a Call to Action is)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; what's the one thing you'd ask a room to do? Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; what a Call to Action is - advocacy vs a sales pitch (distinct from a venture pitch); the Problem/Stakes/Story/Ask shape. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; draft your Problem and your Ask; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - what makes a room *care*? Vulnerability over polish; the story under the argument. Bridge: find your story.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 26 - DEEPEN (Problem -> Stakes -> Story -> Ask)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; write your Stakes (why it matters, to whom); Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; story-craft games - how an ordinary experience becomes a story that moves people. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; write your Story (why it broke *your* heart, what you did); Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - shape the whole arc: Problem -> Stakes -> Story -> Ask; where does it land, where does it lose the room? Bridge: full first draft.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Weeks 27-28 - APPLY (build the talk)
Each week, same shape:
- **Mon / Wed - Self-guided** - build and revise the talk; finalize the project; core; Log; check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; talk-building, delivery practice (low-stakes), the alternative shapes offered. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - revision as care; read a passage aloud (voluntary); hold nerves warmly; vulnerability is the force. Bridge: toward rehearsal.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 29 - APPLY (rehearse in the tribe's safety)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; polish the talk / your chosen shape; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; rehearse in the tribe (safe, warm); confirm each learner's chosen shape (full room / tribe only / recorded / one person). *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; final prep; a Log entry on the year; check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - dry-run in the tribe; hold nerves; the guide holds testimony over applause. Bridge: rest before the feast.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 30 - HARVEST (the Call to Action + feast)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; final touches; rest. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; set the room for the feast; last nerves held. *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - final prep; rest. Check-in.
- **Thu - THE CALL TO ACTION + FEAST** - a real celebration with a real audience: each learner makes the case (as much, and in whatever shape, they choose). Advocacy, not a judged presentation; the cohort cheers each other. *Being seen is a gift, never a demand.* This is The Forge's harvest.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 7 and the Forge year.**

## The year-close (carry the Year-1 evoked-polish)
Before Open Waters: a **co-authoring close** - the learner reviews the year's design *with* the guide ("here's how The Forge was built, and why; tell me what to change for The Helm"); a **Captain's Log ownership handoff** ("it's yours; take it; no copy kept"). **Bridge into Year 3 (The Helm):** *"You found what's worth solving and acted on it. Next: what do you do when there is no easy answer?"* And for any graduating captain, the **Next Great Adventure** rite (off-cycle, the true end of their line).

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
