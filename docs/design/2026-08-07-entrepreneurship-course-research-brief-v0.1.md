## The question

What does it take to build something people are genuinely better for - and what does startup / hustle
culture sell instead? Entrepreneurship as **service** (find a real need, fill it, make it real) vs
entrepreneurship as **extraction** (manufacture a need, capture attention, scale at all costs).

## READ THIS FIRST - most of the firewall is already done

The 2026-07-31 money-books research already did the entrepreneurship core:
- **Wardlaw's *Find a Need and Fill It* (1958)** is the honest anchor - "everything one does is motivated
  by the realization of a need and the desire to fill it." Already the fleet's entrepreneurship book.
- **The extraction fork is already named:** find-a-need-and-fill-it (honest) vs manufacture / inflame a
  need = Karbo's *Lazy Man's Way to Riches* / Hill's fabricated method - "sell relief from the anxiety
  you amplified."
- **The honest test is already written:** does it leave someone with a **capacity** they didn't have, or
  only a **feeling**? The tell is the promise about effort.

So run the **admission gate expecting it to pass**: a single deep read + the money report likely answers
the question, and **no graph is needed.** That is the correct, disciplined outcome - log "graph not
needed" and ship the course from the single pass. Do NOT build a graph to look rigorous.

## What is genuinely NEW (only escalate to a graph for THESE, and only if single-pass can't verify them)

1. **The mechanics of making** - build → ship → iterate. The money research is about earning / serving,
   not the craft of making a thing real (MVP / iteration - evidence-backed vs founder-myth).
2. **Value-creation vs value-extraction ethics** - does the venture make people better off, or
   capture / addict / extract? (Attention economy, dark patterns, addictive-by-design - the exact thing
   Evoked refuses.) This is the entrepreneurship firewall's fresh edge.
3. **Service vs hustle-culture** - entrepreneurship-as-service vs grind / scale-at-all-costs / "crush it"
   ideology; the survivorship bias in founder lore (the money report already caught survivorship bias).
4. **Kid entrepreneurship, developmentally** - the honest lemonade-stand (make something, serve a real
   customer) vs the "kidfluencer / monetize-your-childhood" trap. What is age-real at ~8 vs ~15.

## The lens

All of it, find the truth. Before-they-made-it admissions outrank founder-stage mythology (the way
before-they-were-rich admissions outranked Davos signaling).

## The Evoked firewall (the money firewall, extended to ventures)

The honest test at the business level: **does the venture serve a real need, or manufacture / capture
one?** Does it leave customers with a good they're better for - or an addiction / anxiety / extraction?
The tell: is the engine "fill a need" or "capture attention / manufacture want / lock in"? **A venture
whose engine is manufactured need or captured attention is Karbo at company scale.** This is Evoked's own
product ethic - evoke, not extract - applied to teaching kids to build.

## Child / dev constraints

- Capacity, not "be your own boss / get rich / build a startup." Entrepreneurship = making something that
  serves a real need. Beneficence is native (a business is a way to serve).
- No hustle-culture / grind / "crush it" glorification (same shape as the money-ethics "millionaire" ban);
  no monetize-everything framing for children.
- At ~8: make one thing, serve one real customer (the honest micro-venture). At ~15: the fuller
  build → ship → iterate + the value-vs-extraction ethic.
- Salus + Jake developmental pass; 12-18 register first; 8-11 young version = Wave-2.
