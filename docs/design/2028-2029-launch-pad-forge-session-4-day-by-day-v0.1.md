# Launch Pad Year 2 (The Forge) - Session 4 "The Wider Field" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft | **When:** weeks 10-12 (short session, 3 weeks)
**Companions:** Year 2 studio-year plan (Forge); Forge Sessions 1-3; three-year spec (Season Two)

## The session at a glance
- **Arc:** OPEN (lift your eyes to the world) -> DEEPEN ("what breaks your heart?") -> APPLY (an expedition; the Problem Gallery) -> HARVEST (commit to the world-problem you'll carry through S5-7).
- **The thread:** *the finding question is not "what is important?" but "what breaks your heart?"* (Buechner: where your deep gladness meets the world's deep hunger).
- **Lighter-Forge week:** Tue guided-lighter (the Problem Gallery + the expedition - out in the world), Thu guided-deep (the "what breaks your heart" Socratic + stakeholder work). Mon/Wed self-guided; Fri fallow.

## Care points
- **Safety (BINDING):** world problems can drown a teenager in despair (eco-anxiety, doom). **Always pair the wider field with agency** - you do not have to solve all of it; you pick your slice. Hope, not helplessness. The expedition should leave them *lit up, not crushed.*
- **The expedition is off-site** - toddler logistics differ from a normal guide day: the single-morning coverage travels, or a caregiver comes along, or the expedition is scheduled where coverage is easiest. Plan it per outing.
- **Grief for the world is honored, not bypassed** (Mae): if a learner comes grieving the world, name it as legitimate and sit with it *before* redirecting to action ("grief is love with nowhere to go; now - where can we let it go?"). Witnessed, never excavated - route anything heavy to the SSC (Salus/Bashir).
- Deficit-guard and coach-not-therapist carry.

## Week 10 - OPEN (the wider field)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; notice what in the world you can't stop thinking about; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; **love before heartbreak** (Mae): begin with *"what do you love about being alive on this planet?"* - gladness before hunger, because agency cures powerlessness but only love cures despair, and you fight for what you love, not against what you hate. Then **the Problem Gallery** - a walk past real-world problems (UN goals, local issues, environmental, social); which one won't let you go? *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; sit with the ones that pulled you; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - *"what breaks your heart?"* - not what you *should* care about, what actually moves you. Pair every heartbreak with agency (your slice, not all of it). Bridge: come to the expedition ready to see one firsthand.
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 11 - DEEPEN (the expedition)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; research a problem that pulled you; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter): THE EXPEDITION** - go where a real problem lives (a shelter, a food bank, a council meeting) - **and at least one living-system site that is *not* a scene of failure** (an old-growth stand, a wetland, a farm at harvest, a dark night sky): you are a thread in a living whole, not a technician above a broken machine. The **membership question** (Ibn/Vuna): *"how is this problem already touching your life? Where are you inside this system, not above it?"* See it firsthand. *Off-site; toddler coverage travels/planned.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; write what the expedition stirred; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep)** - debrief the expedition; **stakeholder intro** (who actually *has* this problem?); re-anchor hope-not-helplessness. Bridge: which problem will you carry?
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth.**

## Week 12 - APPLY -> HARVEST (commit to the problem)
- **Mon - Self-guided** - core; narrow toward the problem you'll carry through S5-7; map its stakeholders; Log. Check-in.
- **Tue - Guided (lighter)** - Impossible Question; share candidate problems; problem-neighbors (who might collaborate). *Toddler-flexible.*
- **Wed - Self-guided** - core; commit; Log. Check-in.
- **Thu - Guided (deep): HARVEST** - each learner **commits to the world-problem they'll carry**, *and* the cohort **deliberates one shared "Commons Problem"** into being - argued together, yielded to, chosen as a *we* (Ibn: a problem you can't face alone teaches asabiyyah by structure, not slogan; it becomes the chorus Call to Action in S7). Hope, not helplessness - you pick your slice; and *"you are one hand in a relay that began before you and continues after you"* (Mae: carry it your distance and pass it well - not "I must solve it"). Voluntary share. *Empty-basket honored: "still searching for mine" is real - a chosen problem can't be forced.* Bridge into Session 5 (The Long Game): *"You've chosen what's worth solving. Next: can a big problem actually be solved - and how do you begin?"*
- **Fri - Fallow / hearth - closes Session 4.**

*"We evoke - we never extract."*
