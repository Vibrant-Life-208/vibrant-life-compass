# Launch Pad Year 1 - Session 6 "Chapter One" - Day by Day

**Status:** v0.1 working draft
**Dates:** Jan 10 - Feb 18, 2028 (weeks 19-24 of Origin) - a 6-week long session
**Companions:** Sessions 1-5 day-by-day; studio-year-plan v0.3; three-year spec

## The session at a glance

- **Arc:** OPEN (become the author) → DEEPEN (find the throughline; choose the load-bearing moments) → APPLY (write Chapter One) → HARVEST (the second distillation - the one-page story-of-self / resume).
- **The thread underneath:** *you are the author of your own story - and shaping it small is not making your life small, it is declaring there are chapters to come.*
- **Lens:** Who am I? (now: the one who shapes the telling). **Joy-flavor:** the craft delight of shaping; the hope of "chapters to come."
- **The reframe that makes it land** (Janeway called this the strongest craft in the spec): to a 16-year-old, this *is* their whole life - so don't argue with that, flip it. **"Chapter One" is an act of hope - it declares there are chapters coming.** It doesn't make their life small; it makes their future big.
- **The five-region tie:** the throughline is the **Voice** region at work (the authoring self, first felt in Session 3); the resume distillation touches **Making** (the practical, vocational form).

## The truth-discipline (teach it explicitly - it is the session's safety)

**We never cut to hide. We cut to focus.** What you leave out of Chapter One is not denied or erased - it is *kept*, in the full draft, in the Captain's Log. It is still true. It just isn't load-bearing for *this* chapter's shape. Cutting is choosing what matters most right now - Session 3's power of choice, turned into craft. This reframe is what makes "shrinking" a life feel like authorship, not erasure - hold it front and center, because cutting one's own life can otherwise feel like loss.

## The shrink mechanic (the Guide holds this)
1. From the full draft, find the **throughline** - "what is this chapter *about*?" (One sentence.)
2. Choose the **3-5 load-bearing moments** that set up who you are becoming. ***Keep, because*** - not *cut, because.*
3. Write **Chapter One** - tight, shaped, true.
4. Everything cut goes into the **Captain's Log** - kept, honored, not lost.
5. Distill the *same life* into a **second shape: the one-page story-of-self / resume.** Same material, two forms - a *story* (meaning) and a *page* (practical). Two answers to "who are you," for two different rooms. *(The resume is a sovereignty tool - one true page inside the portfolio, never the season's reduction.)*
