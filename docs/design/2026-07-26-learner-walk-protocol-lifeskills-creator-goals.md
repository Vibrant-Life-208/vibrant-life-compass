# Learner-Walk Verification Protocol - Life Skills & Creator Mindset goals

**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Owner:** Salus (SSC) - lead. With Comes (Journey) + Troi (Developmental).
**Facilitated by:** Europa, with a consented young learner.
**Gate this clears:** Salus's standing condition 7 - no learner-facing surface is verified until a real learner walks it. The goal-decomposition walk is *already live in prod*; this protocol verifies what already ships.

---

## The one question

Does asking a learner to set a **goal** for a Creator Mindset *disposition* (growth mindset, emotional regulation, curiosity, responsibility) **work - or distort the thing?** And does the Life Skills **WOOP** land as a real plan, or as homework?

Everything below serves that question. This is not a full-climb walk; it is scoped to two moments.

---

## Prerequisite (Salus - non-negotiable)

- A **consented young learner**. The adult test cohort (you + both Jennas + Wes) can verify *flow*, but not the *developmental* question - a disposition-goal reads differently to a 12-year-old than to an adult.
- **Parental/guardian consent on the record** before any observation of a child.
- The child can **stop at any point**, no reason needed. The Prime Directive applies to the walker most of all.

---

## Setup

- Learner on their own device, walking live prod.
- Observer present but **quiet**. You are watching, not teaching. No prompting, no rescuing, no explaining what they "should" write.
- Capture = **note-taking only**. No recording of the child unless separately, explicitly consented.
- Note the **time** of any stall (where they stop and for how long).

---

## Moment 1 - Life Skills WOOP (a *capacity* goal)

The learner picks a life skill (leadership / entrepreneurship / financial literacy / wellness) and breaks it into a goal: setup wish -> obstacle -> if-then plan -> what success looks like.

**Watch for:**
- Do they fill it, or stall on a field?
- Does *"what could get in the way - not a prediction, just something worth noticing"* land, or does it invite anxiety?
- Does *"by the end of this year"* feel real, or abstract?
- Is the success line a genuine picture, or a performance ("get better at leadership")?

**The tell:** a real plan reads **specific and theirs**. Homework reads **generic**.

---

## Moment 2 - Creator Mindset disposition-goal (THE question)

Watch what happens when the app asks the learner to set or track a goal around growth mindset, emotional regulation, curiosity, or responsibility.

**The face-read (Salus):** confusion, performance, or genuine engagement?

**The category-error tell:** do they write a *disposition as an achievement* - "I will be more curious by June"? If setting the goal makes them **perform** a mindset rather than **grow** one, that is the signal the model is wrong here.

**The counter-signal:** does the learner gravitate to the **Emotional Regulation tools** (box breathing, 5-4-3-2-1, move-it-out) and treat *those* as the real thing, while the "goal" feels hollow? If practice lands where a goal doesn't, that is evidence dispositions want **practice, not targets**.

---

## Do-not-lead prompts (only if the learner asks *you* what to do)

- "There's no wrong answer - put whatever feels true."
- "You can leave it open if none of it is calling you."
- **Never:** "You should set a goal to..." / "Try to be more..."

---

## Safety (Salus)

- If a disposition-goal - especially emotional regulation - surfaces distress or a hard feeling, **stop the observation and be present.** The child comes before the data.
- On the Connection page / any "who do you matter with" moment, watch for a lonely-learner pinch. **Do not probe.**
