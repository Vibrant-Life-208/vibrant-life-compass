## Part 1 - The shape of the year

### The cycle, and where Year 1 sits

Launch Pad is a three-year expedition run as a cycle: **Origin** (Who am I?) - **The Forge** (What is worth solving?) - **The Helm** (What do I do when there is no easy answer?). This year runs **Origin**.

### The weekly rhythm

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-guided** | **Guide-led** | **Self-guided** | **Guide-led** | **Fallow / hearth** |
| Learner is their own manager | Socratic depth, conversation, group challenge | Learner is their own manager | Socratic depth, conversation, group challenge | Protected. Never filled - for learners *and* the Guide. |

Each studio day runs **9:00-1:30**. On guide days, the **Morning Steward** holds 9:00-11:30 (walk-in, self-work supervision) and Erin leads from 11:30 (see Part 6). *Note the day-swap from the three-year spec (Launch days were Mon/Wed there, Tue/Thu here) - to be reconciled in the guide spec so the docs do not drift.*

### The two day-types

| | **Self-guided - Mon & Wed** | **Guide-led - Tue & Thu** |
|---|---|---|
| Who runs it | The learner (their own manager); a present adult in the room | The Guide holds the room |
| Core mode | Solo + focused. Getting things done. | Together + deep. Thinking out loud. |
| What happens | Core academics / HS credits or chosen path-work, personal project, the season's writing, Captain's Log | Socratic questioning, real conversation, group challenges |
| Adult presence | An adult is **present** (Morning Steward or Guide), not directing. "Present," not merely "reachable." | The Guide, fully present |

### Cohort size

**4-6 learners, target 5.** Below 4, the year cannot fund its own help without the founder subsidizing it with unpaid labor. Above 6, the load outruns one Guide plus a morning assistant, and the Socratic depth (held silence, drawing out the quiet voice, per-learner plan review) degrades into crowd-management. Seed the founding cohort small on purpose - the first cohort becomes the lineage that makes Years 2 and 3 work. Grow in Year 2 once the operating model has proven it holds.

## Part 2 - Equipping the self-manager

The hard truth stands: **you cannot hand a 16-year-old two free days and expect self-management to appear.** And the corollary the reviewers insisted on: **the scaffold must fit the learner, not the calendar.**

### Readiness-gated phases (not date-gated)

The three phases (structured → guided freedom → real autonomy) are **paced to each learner, not to the S-number.** A learner with strong self-regulation may run autonomous by October; a learner with ADHD, anxiety, or a disrupted school history may need structured support into spring - and that is their pace, not their failure.

| Phase | The self-guided day feels like... | Guide's posture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 - Structured | Clear template, required plan, visible checklists, in-the-moment support | Reviews the plan; teaches the habit |
| 2 - Guided freedom | Learner drafts their own week; partners active | Spot-checks; coaches; steps back |
| 3 - Real autonomy | Learner runs their own weeks | Only if asked - and the stepping-back is **named**, so autonomy arrives as trust bestowed, not support withdrawn |

**A permanent-scaffold path is legitimate.** Some learners need the template, the checklist, or a present body-double all year. That is not remediation - it is a wheelchair, and you do not remove a wheelchair in month seven to teach walking. The scaffold may stay, without shame, without it reading as "behind."

### In-the-moment support (not just planning-ahead)

Planning on Thursday does nothing at 9:20 Monday when a task will not start. So the self-guided day also carries **in-the-moment** executive-function support: a visible "I'm on Block 1" board, an optional check-in bell, body-doubling (working alongside a partner or the present adult), and a **lowered help threshold** - the old "reachable if truly stuck" is replaced by an adult who is *present* and whom a learner does not have to prove desperation to approach.

### The weekly planning loop (with asymmetric Bridges)

The guide-led days feed the self-guided days through the **Bridge** (last 20 min of every Tue/Thu Launch):
- **Thursday's Bridge** → plans **Monday**. Because Monday's plan must survive the gap (Friday fallow + weekend), the Thursday Bridge writes a **re-entry note to the future self**: *"When you read this Monday: here's where I was, and the first physical thing to do is ____."*
- **Tuesday's Bridge** → plans **Wednesday** (one day warm; can be lighter).

### The learner's toolkit

1. **The Weekly Plan** - operational planner, separate from the private Captain's Log. **The learner keeps their plans; the Guide reviews in the moment and retains nothing longitudinal.** No year-long adherence ledger.
2. **The Credit Map** (or the equivalent for a learner on a different path) - see Part 4. Learner-owned; the Guide witnesses.
3. **Deep-work cycles** - taught explicitly, on a dated day *before* the first solo day (see Part 5), and ramped (start with a 45-min block, build to 90).
4. **The Stuck Protocol** (posted, in accessible layout - see Appendix).
5. **A rotating accountability partner** - support and presence, not surveillance. Partners rotate by season, so by year's end each learner has been truly seen by several others.

### Accountability without surveillance - and without a self-deficit ledger

- The Guide reviews **the plan and the learner's own reflection**, never raw output, and keeps no running file of who-did-what (refusal #6).
- The **"what got in the way?"** field is reframed to **"what did today actually need from me?"** and does not persist - so twice-weekly honesty does not accumulate into a private ledger of shortfall.
- **Peer-partner guardrail:** the partner is support, never a reporter. A partner never reports on a partner to the Guide; either learner may pass any day; the check-in is coached as *"how are you, and what do you need?"* - compassion, not comparison.
- **A season-level flex valve:** beyond day-flex and week-flex, the Guide may **renegotiate a learner's whole plan** in a hard stretch (the hard *February*), initiated by the Guide so the struggling learner does not have to raise their hand and say "I'm drowning."

## Part 3 - The day templates

### The self-guided day - beginner version (Phase 1)

| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 9:00-9:20 | **Re-entry ritual** (not plan-execution). Re-read the plan: does it still fit? Change what doesn't. Find your thread - where exactly did you leave off - and name the *first physical action*. On Mondays, **check in with your partner first**, then re-enter. |
| 9:20-10:05 | Deep-work Block 1 - core academics / credits or path-work. (Ramp: 45 min early in the year, building to 90.) |
| 10:05-10:20 | Move / break. Off the screen. |
| 10:20-11:50 | Deep-work Block 2 - personal project + the season's writing. |
| 11:50-12:20 | Optional peer collaboration or continue. |
| 12:20-1:15 | **Witnessed quiet** - 15 min of protected, in-room time with nothing asked (Guinan's "empty cup"): not journaling, not planning, just being. Then continue or close. |
| 1:15-1:30 | Close. Captain's Log capture (private), tick the plan (learner keeps it), check out with partner, set tomorrow's seed. |

*Autonomous learners (Phase 3) compress the re-entry and run the blocks themselves. The template ramps down as readiness grows - the beginner's day and the graduate's day are genuinely different, not the same day with more Guide-review layered on.*

### The guide-led day - 9:00-1:30

| Time | Block | Held by |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00-10:00 | Walk-in & Project Hour - soft, self-directed start | **Morning Steward** |
| 10:00-11:00 | Self-work / reading / partner check-ins continue | **Morning Steward** |
| 11:00-11:30 | Transition; Erin arrives from Tots | Steward → Guide handoff |
| 11:30-11:50 | Opening Circle - body-first settle + a *varied* "how are you, really?" | Guide |
| 11:50-12:05 | The Quest Frame - open the quest as a question | Guide |
| 12:05-12:45 | The Table - a shared meal (see inclusion floor below) | Guide (seated, eating, same food) |
| 12:45-1:10 | The Work of the Day - Socratic depth / group challenge | Guide |
| 1:10-1:30 | The Bridge + a *varied* Glad Close | Guide |

*Credit-audit 1:1s do NOT happen in the year's second morning (see Part 5) and are held warmly after belonging is rooted, not as an intake.*

**Going deep - three modes:** Socratic questioning (hold the silence; the first to talk should rarely be you); real conversation; group challenges. Discussion culture (set Week 1): pass is always allowed; disagree with the idea, never the person; "I changed my mind" is a win; the quietest voice gets drawn out on purpose.

**Access - multiple doors *in*, not just the "pass" exit:**
- **Write-first-then-share** think-time (everyone writes 2 min, then speaks) equalizes processing speed.
- At least one **non-verbal contribution channel** every guide day (a written wall, a placed object, art) so the anxious or selective-mute learner has a path *in*.
- Every kinetic activity reads **"place yourself,"** never "stand," with an equal seated/low-mobility path.
- The settle question is a **family of questions** - vary it; when "how are you really" starts getting performed answers, that is the signal to ask it differently. A learner who passes *every* week is noticed privately and warmly, never called out in circle.

### The Table - an inclusion floor, not a lunch break

The Table is the belonging engine, and a shared meal is exclusionary if done carelessly. So:
- **The base meal is always provided, always enough, no contribution ever required to eat.** No cover charge for the food-insecure learner. (The Lineage potluck is additive and celebratory - never the everyday floor.)
- **Allergen / cultural / religious safety planned in advance** - real food this learner can gladly eat, at the same table, never a sad separate plate.
- **Presence, not consumption.** You belong at the Table whether you eat a lot, a little, or nothing. No one watches anyone's plate. (This is a specific reason the Salus + Jake walk matters - the daily meal needs an eating-disorder-safety pass.)
- **The Guide eats, seated, the same food.** A guide who circulates and supervises has made it a cafeteria; a guide who sits and eats has made it a family table.
- **Ritual bones:** a threshold that marks "we are now at table," a rotating small role (someone sets it, someone notices who's missing), screen-free as a gift not a rule, and occasionally food-with-a-story (S2 Lineage DNA spread across the year).

### Holding the tribe across the four non-guide days

Belonging built Day 1 starves by October if not fed. So:
- **Rotating accountability partners** are the tribe-weaving loom - the thread of "we" that survives Mon/Wed/Fri.
- A **"how's the *we*?"** beat opens Tuesday's Circle - a shared thread, an inside reference, a win from someone's solo day the tribe gets to celebrate.
- **Friday is hearth, not only fallow:** an optional, never-attendance-tracked open door some Fridays (a pot on the stove, whoever wants to come). The optionality is the point; the door being *there* makes it a hearth.
- **Re-run the Constellation** at the midpoint and near the Unveiling, so learners physically see how the tribe moved across the year.

## Part 4 - High school core credits (for the learner who chooses it)

**For a learner who names core-credit completion as high on their journey, Year 1 is the year to finish - so Years 2 and 3 are freed for the Forge and the Helm.** (Conditional, not a blanket "must" - the rule applies only to the learner who has made it their own goal.)

### Sovereignty, made structural

- **The goal is re-askable, not once-at-intake.** At every monthly checkpoint the question returns: *"Is this still yours?"* - not only *"are you on track?"* A 16-year-old's August "yes" is not binding on their February self.
- **The "different path" learner gets a designed studio, not a hole.** If a learner is on a genuinely different path, their Block 1 is *their chosen path-work* (an apprenticeship focus, a body of personal projects, a portfolio) - a real, named experience, not the blank left where the credit machine would be.
- **A dignified multi-year path exists.** For a learner whose pace is a function of a learning difference (dyslexia, dyscalculia, processing speed) or a hard season, core credits taking more than one year is *not* falling behind - it is the honest pace of their particular mind. A two-year credit plan is just as valid, named as a right, not a favor.

### The mechanics

1. **Credit audit - first two weeks** (not Day 2 morning). Held warmly, after belonging is rooted. Builds the Credit Map.
2. **Pacing plan** - remaining credits across the year's academic runway, front-loaded in fall.
3. **Monthly checkpoint** - witnessed, never graded; reframed from pass/fail "on track?" to *"what pace is real for you right now, and does the plan match it?"* Adjust the plan to the learner, never the learner to the plan. First glance in September (light), formal at the S4 reflection (Nov).
4. **Reality check, named early and gently** - a learner starting far behind may need a multi-year plan or summer work; an honest, relational conversation, never a week-one efficiency intake.

### DAY 3 - Wednesday, August 18, 2027 - a NARRATED self-guided day

Every first day back from a weekend is a small version of the re-entry problem; every first day back from **Thanksgiving, Winter, and Spring break** is the large version. Each break is followed by a **guide-led re-thread**: "where were you, where are you" - especially critical inside the two-break span of S5 (the Full Draft), where a learner can lose the throughline of their own autobiography across a two-week gap.

## Part 6 - Staffing & sustainability (new)

*The founder decision: run 2027, keep the 9:00 start, fund a named morning adult. This part gives Locked Decision #6 its teeth.*

### The Morning Steward (fills the 9:00-11:30 gap)

Erin runs the Tots until 11:30. So a **funded, named Morning Steward** holds the Launch Pad room 9:00-11:30 on studio days - a *present, non-directing adult* who runs the walk-in, supervises self-work and reading, and is the reachable body for the self-guided days. This is a **lower-skill, lower-cost role** than a teaching co-guide (supervised presence, not Socratic facilitation), which widens who can fill it.

- **Cost floor:** ~$6,000-7,600/yr (a paid assistant, ~2.5 hrs × 4 days × ~30 weeks, rural Idaho, W-2 with overhead). Reducible via a **college education-practicum placement** (supervised field hours) or a **shared adult with the Tots program** where genuine slack exists.
- **Never rest safety-critical daily coverage on volunteers.** A parent-volunteer rota is acceptable only as *backup depth*, never as the floor. Worker classification (W-2 vs 1099) → confirm with counsel.

### The backup bench - two names, never one

One backup is a second single-point-of-failure. Stand up **at least two** oriented adults who can hold either program's floor at supervised-presence level (hold the room and run self-work - not fake Socratic depth).

### The substitute protocol - written, posted, drilled once before Aug 16

- **Guide-led day with Erin out → it converts to a self-guided day.** Learners already know how to run one. A bench adult holds presence; the Socratic depth is **postponed, not faked** (faking it breaks refusal #3).
- **Self-guided day with the present adult out →** the other bench name is on call.
- **The newborn is a scheduled certainty, not a risk.** Pre-designate each term's higher-risk weeks (recovery, appointments) and pre-arrange coverage rather than scrambling.
- **Two-deep floor for minors:** any medical/behavioral/safety event needs one adult on the event and one holding the room. Confirm the real duty-of-care requirement for adult presence with minors with counsel - "reachable" is not "present."

### Locked Decision #6 with a number

Sustainability is a threshold, not a sentiment:
- **The Guide's Friday is fallow too** - protected, not "off-the-floor-but-reachable." A studio that protects the learners' fallow day and not the Guide's has not understood its own principle.
- **A standing supervision / peer connection** - a regular reflective session with someone outside the room (even a monthly call with other microschool guides).
- **A written pause-trigger:** *if the Guide covers both programs solo past [N] consecutive guide days without bench relief, the cohort pauses to fallow-mode until relief is restored.* (Set N with Erin.)

### The economics that close it

At target tuition (Erin sets the real local number), **4-6 learners funds the Morning Steward floor AND a real - if modest - line for Erin's own guide time** (not $0; a studio the founder runs for free is a subsidy with a burnout expiration date). Below 4, the founder subsidizes it. This must not be funded on the founder's unpaid labor or the family's stability (Stephen's income is the floor).

### Precondition to open (binding)

**Year 1 does not open until the Morning Steward is funded and named with a real owner, the two-name backup bench exists, and the substitute protocol is drilled once.** A precondition is satisfied before the door opens, not hoped for after.

```
     [ ] credits/core  [ ] my project  [ ] the season's writing
     [ ] reading  [ ] other: __________

  ---- WEDNESDAY (self-guided) - planned at Tuesday's Bridge ----
  (same shape; Wednesday's plan can be lighter - it's only a day old)

  FRIDAY is fallow / hearth. Do not plan work here.

  STUCK?  (left-aligned, one step per line, also posted large on the wall)
     1. Try it a different way for 10 minutes.
     2. Check your notes and resources.
     3. Ask your partner.
     4. Park it - move to another task, come back.
     5. Still stuck? Write it down for the next guide day.
     Being stuck is fine. Staying stuck in silence is the only mistake.
```
